ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Gideon Ephratah has been a Christian since 1984. He is joined to his wife and best friend of 39 years. He has one daughter and four sons. An avid student of the Bible, he is passionate to let the Scriptures speak for themselves.

To accomplish this he uses certain disciplines and maxims designed to deliver an accurate and precise understanding of God's inspired Word:

  1. Knowing this first, that no prophecy of the scripture is of any private interpretation. — 2 Pet 1:20
  2. Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth. — 2 Tim 2:15
  3. Let God be true, but every man a liar. — Rom 3:4
  4. Stand ye in the ways, and see, and ask for the old paths, where is the good way, and walk therein, and ye shall find rest for your souls. — Jer 6:16
  5. Earnestly contend for the faith which was once delivered unto the saints. — Jude 1:3
  6. For I have not shunned to declare unto you all the counsel of God. — Acts 20:27

Beyond these principles he stresses confirming the CONTEXT of each verse, and accepting nothing as DOCTRINE except that which is established by two or more references. He uses every tool available for Bible study — concordances, dictionaries, atlases, multiple translations (though he prefers the world's oldest texts by default), Bible software, and AI.

It is his earnest prayer that you will receive his work — but not without prayer, scriptural confirmation, and study like the Bereans, to determine whether these things are so.
