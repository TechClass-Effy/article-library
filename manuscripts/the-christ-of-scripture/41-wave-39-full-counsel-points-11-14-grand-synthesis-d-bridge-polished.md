## **POINT 11 — CONFORMED TO THE IMAGE OF THE SON**

### **Romans 8:29:**
*"For those which he knew before, he also predestinate *(predestinated)* to be made like to the image of his Son, that he might be the first born among many brethren."*

### **2 Corinthians 3:18:**
*"But we all behold as in a mirror the glory of the Lord with open face, and are changed into the same image, from glory to glory, as by the Spirit of the Lord."*

---

### Observation

**The believer is being conformed to the image of the Son.** This is the Father's predestined purpose. We who were in Adam's fallen image are being remade in the Son's glorious image — **from glory to glory** — by the Spirit.

The Son is the **πρωτότοκος ἐν πολλοῖς ἀδελφοῖς** — *"the firstborn among many brethren."* He is the prototype; we are being conformed to His pattern. He has many brethren — and we are among them.

---

## **POINT 12 — SHARERS IN HIS SUFFERINGS AND GLORY**

### **Philippians 3:10-11:**
*"That I may know him, and the virtue of his resurrection, and the fellowship of his afflictions, and be made conformable unto his death, If by any means I might attain unto the resurrection of the dead."*

### **Romans 8:17** — already cited: *"if so be that we suffer with him, that we may be also glorified together."*

### **1 Peter 4:13:**
*"But rejoice, inasmuch as ye are partakers of Christ's sufferings, that when his glory shall appear, ye may be glad and rejoice."*

---

### Observation

The believer shares **the fellowship of Christ's sufferings** (κοινωνία τῶν παθημάτων αὐτοῦ). Not that we contribute to atonement (Christ's suffering was once-for-all), but that we participate in the kind of suffering He endured — opposition, rejection, persecution, the cross-bearing life — **in fellowship with Him.**

And this fellowship in suffering is the gateway to **fellowship in glory.** What the Son endured, He endured **for us**; what we endure, we endure **with Him**.

---

## **POINT 13 — APPEARING WITH HIM IN GLORY**

### **Colossians 3:4:**
*"When Christ, which is our life, shall appear, then shall ye also appear with him in glory."*

### **1 John 3:2:**
*"...but we know that when he shall be made manifest, we shall be like him: for we shall see him as he is."*

### **1 Thessalonians 4:17:**
*"...and so shall we ever be with the Lord."*

---

### Observation

The final identification: when the Son appears in glory, **we appear with Him in glory.** We shall be **like Him** — for we shall see Him as He is. And **we shall ever be with the Lord.**

This is the consummation of identification: from co-crucifixion to co-glorification, from being hidden with Christ in God to being manifested with Him in glory. The entire arc is one continuous identification.

---

## **POINT 14 — REIGNING WITH HIM**

### **2 Timothy 2:12:**
*"If we suffer, we shall also reign together with him..."*

### **Revelation 20:6:**
*"...but they shall be the Priests of God and of Christ, and shall reign with him a thousand yeere *(years)*."*

### **Revelation 22:5:**
*"...and they shall reign for evermore."*

---

### Observation

The believer shall **reign with Christ.** Not as His equals, but as those joined to Him — sharing in His kingdom. **Co-kings under the King of kings**, **co-priests under the Great High Priest** — in the consummation.

## **WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES — THE ULTIMATE COHERENCE**

The believer is identified with Christ **in every redemptive act of the Son** — past, present, and future. There is no facet of the Son's saving work in which we do not participate by virtue of being **in Him.**

**This is not metaphor.** Paul does not say *"as if we were crucified with Him."* He says *"I have been crucified with Christ"* — perfect tense, real reality. Not a legal fiction, not a poetic figure, but the **actual reckoning of the Father** that includes us in His Son's history and destiny.

**The mechanism:** The Father, who is Eternal Spirit, chose us in His Beloved Son before the foundation of the world. He so identified us with His Son that what the Son did, we did in Him. The Son's death is our death; His burial is our burial; His resurrection is our resurrection; His ascension is our ascension; His seating is our seating; His glory is our glory; His inheritance is our inheritance.

**Therefore:**
- We do not need to add to His work. It is complete.
- We do not need to fear condemnation. We are in Him who is acquitted.
- We do not need to seek our own righteousness. We have His.
- We do not need to seek our own glory. We are joint-heirs of His.
- We do not need to fear death. We have already died with Him.
- We do not need to fear judgment. We have already been justified in Him.

**We are who He is — not in essence, but in identification by the Father's reckoning and the Spirit's union.**

## **GALATIANS 2:20 — REVISITED IN LIGHT OF THE WHOLE**

Now we can hear Paul's testimony with full resonance:

*"I have been crucified with Christ"* — co-crucified, established by Rom 6:6.
*"It is no longer I who live"* — the old man is dead, new creation is the reality (2 Cor 5:17).
*"But Christ lives in me"* — the indwelling of the Son, established by John 14:20, 23; Col 1:27.
*"And the life which I now live in the flesh, I live by faith in the Son of God"* — by present faith we appropriate this identification daily.
*"Who loved me and gave Himself for me"* — the personal, individual love of the Beloved Son for each elect believer.

**This is not Paul alone. This is every believer.** Every elect saint can say with Paul: *"I have been crucified with Christ; I no longer live; Christ lives in me; I live by faith of the Son of God who loved me and gave Himself for me."*

# **A CLOSING OBSERVATION**

Brother, what we have surveyed here is the **richest doctrinal treasure of the New Testament.** The believer's identification in Christ is the basis of:
- Our **security** — we are in Him who cannot be lost.
- Our **standing** — we are accepted in the Beloved.
- Our **sanctification** — we are being conformed to His image.
- Our **service** — we live not for ourselves but for Him who loved us.
- Our **suffering** — we share His cross, knowing it leads to His crown.
- Our **hope** — we shall appear with Him in glory.

Every fear, every doubt, every accusation of the enemy is answered in this single phrase: **"in Christ."**

We are **in Him** — and therefore everything the Father has decreed concerning the Son flows to us by identification. We are not aliens looking on; **we are members of His body, of His flesh, of His bones.** We are not orphans hoping for adoption; **we are children of God, joint-heirs with Christ.** We are not sinners hoping for mercy; **we are crucified with Christ, dead to sin, alive to God in Christ Jesus.**

This is the believer's identification. This is who we are. This is what the Father has done in His Beloved Son.
