## **Daniel 7 — The Son of Man before the Ancient of Days; the (B) Arc Opens**

# **DANIEL 7 — THE SON OF MAN BEFORE THE ANCIENT OF DAYS**

This is the foundational vision of the Son's coming reign, and it is the text Jesus Himself cited as His own (Matt 26:64; Mark 14:62).

### **Daniel 7:9-10, NETS (Old Greek):**

> I kept watching until thrones were set, and an ancient of days sat, having a cloak like snow, and the tuft of hair on his head was like pure wool. The throne was like a flame of fire shooting out, and a stream of fire went out from before him. A thousand thousands were waiting on him, and ten thousand times ten thousand stood attending him. And books were opened, and a court sat in judgment. — **Daniel 7:9-10, NETS (Old Greek)**

*NETS’s Daniel carries Old Greek and Theodotion in parallel columns; the draft cites the LXX voice. Theodotion reads “and his clothing was white like snow, and the hair of his head was like pure wool; his throne was a flame of fire; its wheels were burning fire. A stream of fire drew in before him. A thousand thousands were serving him … A court sat in judgment, and books were opened.” *(Theodotion, the parallel column)* — the Old Greek print has “having a cloak like snow, and the tuft of hair on his head … were waiting on him … And books were opened, and a court sat in judgment.” The draft’s observation Greek κριτήριον ἐκάθισεν, καὶ βίβλοι ἠνεῴχθησαν carries the Theodotion order (a court sat, and books were opened).

### Observations of foundational weight

**(1)** **παλαιὸς ἡμερῶν** — *"the Ancient of Days."* This is **the Father** — the One God, the supreme Judge, enthroned in fire and surrounded by myriads of attendants. Per our foundation: **the Ancient of Days = the Father, the One God of the Shema and Isaiah 44-46.**

**(2)** **θρόνοι ἐτέθησαν** — *"thrones were set up"* (plural). Multiple thrones — preparing for the Son to be brought before the Father to receive His throne.

**(3)** **κριτήριον ἐκάθισεν, καὶ βίβλοι ἠνεῴχθησαν** — *"the court sat, and the books were opened."* The judgment scene is convened. This corresponds to **Revelation 20:11-12** — the great white throne judgment, where books are opened.

## **Daniel 7:13-14, NETS (Old Greek) — The Son of Man Coming**

### **Daniel 7:13-14, NETS (Old Greek):**

> I was watching in the night visions, and lo, as it were a son of man was coming upon the clouds of heaven. And he came as far as the ancient of days, and the attendants were present with him. And royal authority was given to him, and all the nations of the earth according to posterity, and all honor was serving him. And his authority is an everlasting authority, which shall never be removed— and his kingship, which will never perish. — **Daniel 7:13-14, NETS (Old Greek)**

*The same parallel columns; the seat prints the Old Greek. Theodotion reads “coming with the clouds of heaven … and was presented to him. And to him was given the dominion and the honor and the kingship … which will not be destroyed.” *(Theodotion, the parallel column)*. The draft’s observation Greek καὶ ὡς παλαιὸς ἡμερῶν παρῆن is the Theodotion wording the Old Greek print renders “And he came as far as the ancient of days” — recorded when this passage was first seated (the Self-Identifications wave); no change now.

### Observations of supreme importance for our study

**(1)** **ὡς υἱὸς ἀνθρώπου** — *"as a Son of Man."* The figure is described as **like a son of man** — i.e., **in the form of a Man.** This corresponds with everything we have established about the Son: He is the exalted Man Christ Jesus (1 Tim 2:5; Heb 9:24).

**(2)** **ἐπὶ τῶν νεφελῶν τοῦ οὐρανοῦ ἐρχόμενος** — *"coming on the clouds of heaven."* The mode of His coming is on the clouds — fulfilled in:
- Acts 1:9-11 (a cloud received Him; He shall so come)
- Matt 24:30; 26:64
- Rev 1:7

**(3)** **ὡς παλαιὸς ἡμερῶν παρῆν** — *"He came as far as the Ancient of Days."* The Son of Man **approaches** the Ancient of Days. **He is brought *before* the Ancient of Days — He is *not* the Ancient of Days.** This is decisive for our foundation. Two distinct figures: the Father enthroned, and the Son of Man brought before Him.

**(4)** **ἐδόθη αὐτῷ ἐξουσία** — *"authority was given to Him."* **By whom?** The text's structure makes it clear: by the Ancient of Days (the Father). This is the same passive language we have seen throughout — **the Father gives; the Son receives.** Matches Matt 28:18 (*"All authority has been given to Me"*) — explicitly received from the Father.

**(5)** **πάντα τὰ ἔθνη τῆς γῆς... αὐτῷ λατρεύουσα** — *"all the nations of the earth... serving/worshipping Him."* The verb **λατρεύω** is the verb for **divine worship/service** — used of worship rendered to God. The Son of Man receives the divine service of all nations **by the Father's grant**.

This perfectly fulfills the pattern of Isa 45:23 — *"unto Me every knee shall bow"* — which Paul applies to Jesus in Phil 2:10-11, **"to the glory of God the Father."** The worship rendered to the Son is by the Father's will and to the Father's glory.

**(6)** **ἐξουσία αἰώνιος... βασιλεία ἥτις οὐ μὴ φθαρῇ** — *"eternal authority... a kingdom that shall not be destroyed."* The Son's reign, granted by the Father, is everlasting.

### **The Critical NT Cross-Witness — Jesus' Own Use of Daniel 7**

### **Matthew 26:63-64, 1599 Geneva — Jesus’ confession before the high priest:**

> … the chief Priest answered, and said to him, I charge thee swear unto us by the living God, to tell us, If thou be that Christ the Son of God, or no. ... Jesus said to him, Thou hast said it: nevertheless I say unto you, hereafter shall ye see the Son of man, sitting at the right hand of the power of God, and come in the clouds of the heaven. — **Matthew 26:63-64, 1599 Geneva**

*(the draft’s “that Son of man” reads the Geneva’s “the Son of man” — recorded on the bank row when this confession was seated; the draft’s Greek block keeps θρόνῳ/νεφελῶν load-bearing terms in the observations below, and its wording checked word-for-word against the whole-span Greek this wave)*

### Observation

Jesus' confession before the high priest combines:
- **Psalm 110:1** — *"sitting at the right hand of power"*
- **Daniel 7:13** — *"coming on the clouds of heaven"*

These two Old Testament texts form the **double pillar** of His self-claim. He is **both** the Lord at God's right hand (Ps 110) and the Son of Man coming on the clouds (Dan 7).

**Jesus identifies Himself as the figure in Daniel's vision.** This is decisive. The Son of Man before the Ancient of Days = Jesus before the Father.
