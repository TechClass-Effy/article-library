## **The Foundational Confession: The One God**
### **Deuteronomy 6:4 — The Shema**
**LXX:**
*"Ἄκουε, Ἰσραήλ· Κύριος ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν Κύριος εἷς ἐστιν."*
**1599 Geneva:**
*"Hear, O Israel, The Lord our God is Lord only."*
This is the bedrock confession. **One Lord. One God.** Not three. Not a council. Not a
composite. **One.**
---
### **Second witness — from the lips of the Messiah Himself:**
**Mark 12:29 (Greek):**
*"ἀπεκρίθη ὁ Ἰησοῦς ὅτι Πρώτη ἐστίν· Ἄκουε, Ἰσραήλ, Κύριος ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν Κύριος εἷς
ἐστιν."*
**1599 Geneva:**
*"And Jesus answered him, The first of all the commandments is, Hear, Israel, The Lord our
God is the Lord only."*
Jesus Himself — centuries after Moses and yet centuries before Nicaea — aﬃrms the Shema
as the **first** of all commandments. He does not amend it. He does not qualify it. He
confesses **one God**.
---
## **The Father Identified as That One God**
### **Witness #1 — Jesus to the Father:**
**John 17:3 (Greek):**
*"αὕτη δέ ἐστιν ἡ αἰώνιος ζωή, ἵνα γινώσκωσιν σὲ τὸν μόνον ἀληθινὸν Θεὸν καὶ ὃν ἀπέστειλας
Ἰησοῦν Χριστόν."*
**1599 Geneva:**
*"And this is life eternal, that they know thee to be the only very God, and whom thou hast
sent, Jesus Christ."*
The Messiah Himself addresses the Father as **τὸν μόνον ἀληθινὸν Θεὸν** — "the only true
God" — and distinguishes Himself, **Ἰησοῦν Χριστόν**, as the one *sent* by that God. The
Sender and the Sent are not collapsed; the One God is the Father, and Jesus is His Christ.
---
### **Witness #2 — Paul to the Corinthians:**
**1 Corinthians 8:6 (Greek):**
*"ἀλλ᾽ ἡμῖν εἷς Θεὸς ὁ Πατήρ, ἐξ οὗ τὰ πάντα καὶ ἡμεῖς εἰς αὐτόν, καὶ εἷς Κύριος Ἰησοῦς
Χριστός, δι᾽ οὗ τὰ πάντα καὶ ἡμεῖς δι᾽ αὐτοῦ."*
**1599 Geneva:**
*"Yet unto us there is but one God, which is that Father, of whom are all things, and we in him:
and one Lord Jesus Christ, by whom are all things, and we by him."*
Paul, the Apostle, places **ὁ Πατήρ** — the Father — as the **εἷς Θεός**, the *one* God. Jesus
Christ is named **εἷς Κύριος** — *one Lord* — the agent **δι᾽ οὗ** (through whom). The Father
is the Source (**ἐξ οὗ**); the Son is the Mediator/Instrument (**δι᾽ οὗ**).
---
### **Two witnesses established:** The One God of the Shema **is the Father.** Jesus the
Christ is His Sent One, His Anointed, His Lord — distinct from the One God, and subordinate
as the agent to the Source.
---

## **Therefore, our refined foundation:**
1. There is **one God** — Deut. 6:4, confirmed by Jesus in Mark 12:29.
2. That one God is **the Father** — John 17:3, 1 Cor. 8:6.
3. Jesus the Christ is the **Son** of that God, the **sent** one, the **Lord** through whom the
Father acts — distinct from, and having as His own God, the Father.
4. The "Spirit of God" in Genesis 1:2 must therefore be understood not as a separate "person"
of a later-invented triune godhead, but according to its plain Scriptural usage — which we will
examine in due course as **the breath, power, and active presence of the One God Himself**
(cf. Ps 32:6 LXX, where the *word* and the *breath of His mouth* are instrumental expressions
of the one Lord).
---
## **Now — returning to Genesis 1:1 in light of this:**
**LXX:** *"Ἐν ἀρχῇ ἐποίησεν ὁ Θεὸς τὸν οὐρανὸν καὶ τὴν γῆν."*
**ὁ Θεὸς** here is **the Father** — the one God of the Shema. He is the Creator. He is the
Source (**ἐξ οὗ τὰ πάντα**, 1 Cor 8:6).
The question we will need to address carefully — without trinitarian assumptions — is the
relationship of the **Son** to this creative act, given that 1 Cor 8:6 also says **δι᾽ οὗ τὰ πάντα**
("by whom are all things") of Jesus Christ, and that John 1:1-3 speaks of the **Λόγος**.
That investigation belongs to the **Son's** part of the study, and we will undertake it only
*after* the Father — the One God — is firmly established as the foundation.
---
Shall we now widen the foundation of **the One God, the Father**, by examining further OT
witnesses to His singular, monotheistic identity (Isaiah 44-46 being particularly emphatic),
before we turn to who His Only-Begotten Son is?
Yes, let us proceed to Isaiah 44-46 and examine those emphatic declarations of the One God's
singular identity.
Excellent. Isaiah 44-46 contains some of the most thunderous, uncompromising declarations
of monotheism in all of Scripture. The One God speaks for Himself here — in the first person —
and leaves no room for any rival, partner, council, or co-equal.
Let us proceed methodically.
---
## **ISAIAH 44 — "Beside Me there is no God"**
### **Isaiah 44:6**
**LXX:**
*"Οὕτως λέγει ὁ Θεὸς ὁ βασιλεὺς τοῦ Ἰσραὴλ ὁ ῥυσάμενος αὐτὸν Θεὸς σαβαώθ· Ἐγὼ
πρῶτος καὶ ἐγὼ μετὰ ταῦτα, πλὴν ἐμοῦ οὐκ ἔστιν Θεός."*
**1599 Geneva:**
*"Thus saith the Lord the King of Israel and his redeemer, the Lord of hosts, I am the first, and I
am the last, and without me is there no God."*
**Observation:** The speaker — *ὁ Θεὸς*, the King of Israel, the Redeemer, the Lord of Hosts
— declares: **"I am first, and I am after these things; besides Me there is no God."** Not "no
other God like Me," not "no God of equal rank" — **οὐκ ἔστιν Θεός. There is no God.** Full
stop.
---
### **Isaiah 44:8**
**LXX:**
*"μὴ παρακαλύπτεσθε· οὐκ ἀπ᾽ ἀρχῆς ἠνωτίσασθε καὶ ἀπήγγειλα ὑμῖν; μάρτυρες ὑμεῖς ἐστε εἰ
ἔστιν Θεὸς πλὴν ἐμοῦ· καὶ οὐκ ἦσαν τότε."*
**1599 Geneva:**
*"Fear ye not, neither be afraid: have not I told thee of old, and have declared it? ye are even
my witnesses, whether there be a God beside me, and that there is no God that I know not."*
**Observation:** The God of Israel calls His people as **μάρτυρες** — *witnesses* — to testify
whether any other God exists. His own answer: **οὐκ ἦσαν** — *they were not, they are not,
they shall not be.*
---
### **Isaiah 44:24**
**LXX:**
*"Οὕτως λέγει Κύριος ὁ λυτρούμενός σε καὶ ὁ πλάσσων σε ἐκ κοιλίας· Ἐγὼ Κύριος ὁ συντελῶν
πάντα ἐξέτεινα τὸν οὐρανὸν μόνος καὶ ἐστερέωσα τὴν γῆν. τίς ἕτερος;"*
**1599 Geneva:**
*"Thus saith the Lord thy redeemer, and he that formed thee from the womb, I am the Lord,
that made all things, that spread out the heavens alone, and stretched out the earth by
myself."*
**Observation of supreme importance for our study:**
The Lord declares: **ἐξέτεινα τὸν οὐρανὸν μόνος** — *"I stretched out the heaven ALONE."*
And **ἐστερέωσα τὴν γῆν** — *"I established/made-firm the earth"* (note the *στερεόω* root —
connecting to **στερέωμα**, the firmament of Gen 1:6; this will matter when cosmology
becomes relevant). And then the challenge: **τίς ἕτερος;** — *"Who else?"*
This single verse forbids any reading of Genesis 1:1 that places a second divine person beside
the Father as co-creator. **The Father stretched out the heavens ALONE.** When we come to
examine the Son's role and the meaning of John 1:3 / Col 1:16 / 1 Cor 8:6 ("through Him"), we
must reconcile those texts with **this** declaration — not the other way around. **God speaks
first; men interpret after.**
---
## **ISAIAH 45 — "I am the Lord, and there is none else"**
### **Isaiah 45:5-6**
**LXX:**
*"ὅτι ἐγὼ Κύριος ὁ Θεός, καὶ οὐκ ἔστιν ἔτι πλὴν ἐμοῦ Θεός, καὶ οὐκ ᾔδεις με, ἵνα γνῶσιν οἱ
ἀπὸ ἀνατολῶν ἡλίου καὶ οἱ ἀπὸ δυσμῶν ὅτι οὐκ ἔστιν πλὴν ἐμοῦ· ἐγὼ Κύριος ὁ Θεός, καὶ οὐκ
ἔστιν ἔτι."*
**1599 Geneva:**
*"I am the Lord, and there is none other, there is no God beside me... That they may know from
the rising of the sun and from the West, that there is none beside me: I am the Lord, and there
is none other."*
**Observation:** Five times in two verses the singularity is hammered: **"I am the Lord, and
there is none else."** This is not the language of a tri-personal being veiling part of His identity.
This is plain, unambiguous, first-person testimony of **one Being, one God**.
---
### **Isaiah 45:18**
**LXX:**
*"Οὕτως λέγει Κύριος ὁ ποιήσας τὸν οὐρανόν — οὗτος ὁ Θεὸς ὁ καταδείξας τὴν γῆν καὶ
ποιήσας αὐτήν, αὐτὸς διώρισεν αὐτήν, οὐκ εἰς κενὸν ἐποίησεν αὐτήν ἀλλὰ κατοικεῖσθαι —
Ἐγώ εἰμι, καὶ οὐκ ἔστιν ἔτι."*
**1599 Geneva:**
*"For thus saith the Lord (that created heaven, God himself, that formed the earth, and made it:
he that prepared it, he created it not in vain: he formed it to be inhabited) I am the Lord, and
there is none other."*
**Observation:** **οὗτος ὁ Θεός** — *"This is the God"* — the One who made the heaven,
formed the earth, prepared it, created it for habitation. **Ἐγώ εἰμι, καὶ οὐκ ἔστιν ἔτι.** *"I AM,
and there is none else."*
Note the **Ἐγώ εἰμι** — the same self-disclosure formula that runs through Isaiah and which
Jesus will later use of Himself (John 8:58). We will mark this and return to it: the question is
*how* the Son can speak with the voice of the One God, when the One God has declared that
there is no other.
---
### **Isaiah 45:21-22**
**LXX:**
*"...τίς ἀνήγγειλεν ταῦτα ἀπ᾽ ἀρχῆς; τότε ἀνηγγέλη ὑμῖν· ἐγὼ ὁ Θεός, καὶ οὐκ ἔστιν ἄλλος
πλὴν ἐμοῦ· δίκαιος καὶ σωτὴρ οὐκ ἔστιν πάρεξ ἐμοῦ. ἐπιστράφητε πρός με καὶ σωθήσεσθε, οἱ
ἀπ᾽ ἐσχάτου τῆς γῆς· ἐγώ εἰμι ὁ Θεός, καὶ οὐκ ἔστιν ἄλλος."*
**1599 Geneva:**
*"...who hath declared this from the beginning? or hath told it of old? Have not I the Lord? and
there is none other God beside me, a just God, and a Saviour: there is none beside me. Look
unto me, and ye shall be saved: all the ends of the earth: for I am God, and there is none
other."*
**Observation:** **δίκαιος καὶ σωτὴρ οὐκ ἔστιν πάρεξ ἐμοῦ** — *"There is no righteous one
and Saviour besides Me."* The Father claims the title **σωτήρ** for Himself, exclusively. This
too will require careful handling when we treat the Son, who is also called Saviour in the New
Testament. The resolution — as we shall see — is *not* by collapsing them into one Being, but
by recognizing the Son as the Father's appointed agent of salvation, executing the will of the
One Saviour.
---
### **Isaiah 45:23**
**LXX:**
*"κατ᾽ ἐμαυτοῦ ὀμνύω· ἦ μὴν ἐξελεύσεται ἐκ τοῦ στόματός μου δικαιοσύνη, οἱ λόγοι μου οὐκ
ἀποστραφήσονται, ὅτι ἐμοὶ κάμψει πᾶν γόνυ καὶ ἐξομολογήσεται πᾶσα γλῶσσα τῷ Θεῷ."*
**1599 Geneva:**
*"I have sworn by my self: the word is gone out of my mouth in righteousness, and shall not
return, That every knee shall bow unto me, and every tongue shall swear by me."*
**Observation of great importance for the Son's study:** The Father swears **by Himself** that
**to Him** every knee shall bow. Paul cites this exact text in Philippians 2:10-11 of Jesus Christ
— but with the climactic note that this bowing is **εἰς δόξαν Θεοῦ Πατρός** — *"to the glory of
God the Father."* The Son receives homage **as the Father's appointed Lord**, and the result
is the Father's glory. The monotheism is preserved.
---

### **Isaiah 46:5**
**LXX:**
*"τίνι με ὡμοιώσατε; ἴδετε τεχνάσασθε, οἱ πλανώμενοι."*
**1599 Geneva:**
*"To whom will ye make me like, or make me equal, or compare me, that I should be like him?"*
**Observation:** The God of Israel asks: **τίνι με ὡμοιώσατε;** — *"To whom have you likened
Me?"* The implicit answer: *no one.* He is incomparable. Any theology that posits another
Person *equal* in being to the Father — co-eternal, co-equal, of the same substance — has
fallen under this rebuke.

## **SUMMARY OF THE FOUNDATION**
From Isaiah 44-46, the One God declares — in His own voice, repeatedly, emphatically, and
without qualification:
| Declaration | Reference |
|---|---|
| "I am the first and the last; besides Me there is no God." | 44:6 |
| "Is there any God besides Me? There is none." | 44:8 |
| "I stretched out the heavens ALONE." | 44:24 |
| "I am the Lord, and there is none else; no God besides Me." | 45:5-6 |
| "I AM, and there is none else." | 45:18 |
| "There is no righteous one and Saviour besides Me." | 45:21 |
| "I have sworn by Myself... to Me every knee shall bow." | 45:23 |
| "I am God, and there is none like Me." | 46:9 |
| "To whom will you liken Me?" | 46:5 |
---
## **The Foundation Established**
1. **The One God is numerically one Being** — not a council, not a triad, not a multiplicity of
persons sharing essence.
2. **He created the heavens and the earth ALONE** (44:24) — this stands as the controlling text
for Genesis 1:1.
3. **He alone is Saviour** (45:21).
4. **To Him alone every knee shall bow** (45:23).
5. **He is incomparable** — no being is *like* Him (46:5, 9).
This is the God whom Jesus called **τὸν μόνον ἀληθινὸν Θεὸν** — *the only true God* (John
17:3) — and whom Jesus called **τὸν Πατέρα μου καὶ Θεόν μου** — *my Father and my God*
(John 20:17).
**The Father is the One God of Isaiah 44-46.** This is our foundation. Any study of the Son
must be built upon — and reconciled with — this declaration, never against it.
