# **PART 6 — WATCHFULNESS AND RESISTANCE OF TEMPTATION**

The believer is in spiritual conflict. The Christian life requires vigilance.

## **The Reality of the Conflict**

### **1 Peter 5:8-9 — Geneva 1599:**

*"Be sober, and watch: for your adversary the devil as a roaring lion walketh about, seeking whom he may devour: Whom resist steadfast in the faith..."*

---

### Observations

**(1) Two commands:** **be sober** (νήψατε — clear-minded, self-controlled) and **watch** (γρηγορήσατε — alert, vigilant).

**(2) The adversary is real** — the devil prowls like a roaring lion seeking prey.

**(3) The response: resist him steadfast in the faith.** The believer **resists** — does not flee or surrender. The resistance is **in the faith** — grounded in the truths of who Christ is and who the believer is in Him.

---

## **The Armor of God**

### **Ephesians 6:10-11 — Geneva 1599:**

*"Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord, and in the power of his might. Put on the whole armor of God, that ye may be able to stand against the assaults of the devil..."*

The full armor:
- **Belt of truth** (v. 14).
- **Breastplate of righteousness** (v. 14).
- **Feet shod with the preparation of the Gospel of peace** (v. 15).
- **Shield of faith** (v. 16).
- **Helmet of salvation** (v. 17).
- **Sword of the Spirit, which is the Word of God** (v. 17).
- **All prayer and supplication in the Spirit** (v. 18).

The believer is to **put on** the full armor and **stand.**

---

## **Resisting Temptation**

### **James 4:7 — Geneva 1599:**

*"Submit yourselves to God: resist the devil, and he will flee from you."*

**Two commands in order:**
1. **Submit to God.**
2. **Resist the devil.**

The order matters. Submission to God is the foundation; resistance to the devil is the result. **The believer who submits to God can resist the devil; the devil will flee.**

### **1 Corinthians 10:13 — Geneva 1599:**

There hath no temptation taken you, but such as appertain *(applies)* to man: and God is faithful, which will not suffer you to be tempted above that you be able, but will even give the issue with the temptation, that ye may be able to bear it.

---

### Observations

**(1) Temptation is common to humanity** — no temptation is unique or unprecedented.

**(2) God is faithful** — He will not allow temptation beyond your ability to bear.

**(3) He provides the way of escape** — *"ἔκβασις"* — the exit. With every temptation, the Father provides a way out, that the believer may be able to bear it.

The believer's responsibility is to **look for the way of escape** and **take it.**

---

## **Practical Implications**

**1. Be alert.** Recognize you are in conflict. The devil seeks to devour. Be watchful.

**2. Put on the armor daily.** Through prayer, Scripture, and conscious dependence on the Lord, equip yourself for battle.

**3. Flee youthful lusts** (2 Tim 2:22). Some temptations are to be resisted; others are to be fled. Sexual temptation, especially, is to be fled. Joseph fled Potiphar's wife (Gen 39:12). The believer does not stay to debate temptation; he flees.

**4. Look for the way of escape.** When temptation comes, ask: *"Father, show me the way of escape."* And then take it.

**5. Submit before resisting.** Submission to God precedes resistance to the devil.

**6. Be especially watchful in seasons of weakness** — fatigue, hunger, isolation, success (which can produce pride), failure (which can produce despair).

**7. Have accountability partners.** Other believers who will ask hard questions and pray with you in your struggles.
