## **Part 3 — Acts Revealing His Authority**

## **3.1 — Authority to Forgive Sins**

### **Mark 2:5-12, 1599 Geneva:**

> … Now when Jesus saw their faith, he said to the sick of the palsy, Son, thy sins are forgiven thee. ... And there were certain of the Scribes sitting there, and reasoning in their hearts, Why doth this man speak such blasphemies? who can forgive sins, but God only? ... But that ye may know, that the Son of man hath authority in earth to forgive sins … — **Mark 2:5-12, 1599 Geneva**

---

### Observation

The scribes correctly perceive that **only God forgives sins.** Jesus does not deny their premise — He demonstrates that **He has been given authority on earth** (**ἐξουσίαν ἔχει**) to do so. The authority is given to **the Son of Man on earth** — i.e., delegated by the Father. This is consistent with our foundation: the Father is the ultimate source; the Son exercises the Father's authority by appointment.

---

## **3.2 — Authority Over Nature**

### **Mark 4:39-41, 1599 Geneva:**

> … And he rose up, and rebuked the wind, and said unto the sea, Peace, and be still. So the wind ceased, and it was a great calm. ... And they feared exceedingly, and said one to another, Who is this, that both the wind and sea obey him? — **Mark 4:39-41, 1599 Geneva**

---

### Observation

The disciples ask the right question: **"Who is this?"** Only the LORD calms the sea (Ps 107:28-29). Jesus' command over wind and sea is a divine prerogative — exercised in person on earth.

---

## **3.3 — Authority to Raise the Dead**

Jesus raises three persons during His ministry:
- **Jairus' daughter** (Mark 5:35-43)
- **The widow's son at Nain** (Luke 7:11-17)
- **Lazarus** (John 11)

### **John 11:25-26, 43-44, 1599 Geneva:**

> Jesus said unto her, I am the resurrection and the life: he that believeth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he live. And whosoever liveth, and believeth in me, shall never die ... As he had spoken these things, he cried with a loud voice, Lazarus, come forth. Then he that was dead, came forth … — **John 11:25-26, 43-44, 1599 Geneva**

---

### Observation

Jesus identifies Himself as **the Resurrection and the Life** — and demonstrates this immediately by raising Lazarus. The act validates the claim. **This is a divine prerogative** — only the LORD gives life (Deut 32:39; 1 Sam 2:6). And yet, **Jesus says elsewhere** (John 5:26): *"For as the Father hath life in himself, so likewise hath he given to the Son to have life in himself."* The Son has been **given** by the Father to have life in Himself. The pattern holds: derivative, delegated, but truly possessed.

---

## **3.4 — Authority to Judge**

### **John 5:22-23, 26-27, 1599 Geneva:**

> For the Father judgeth no man, but hath committed all judgment unto the Son, Because that all men should honor the Son, as they honor the Father ... For as the Father hath life in himself, so likewise hath he given to the Son to have life in himself, And hath given him power also to execute judgment, in that he is the Son of man. — **John 5:22-23, 26-27, 1599 Geneva**

---

### Observation of foundational importance

This is Jesus' own clear statement of the Father-Son order:
- **The Father has committed all judgment to the Son** (**δέδωκεν**).
- The Father **has given the Son** to have life in Himself.
- The Father has **given** Him authority to judge.

**Three times the Father is the Giver; the Son is the Receiver.** This is the consistent biblical pattern, and Jesus Himself articulates it.

But notice also: **"that all men should honor the Son as they honor the Father."** The Son is to receive **equal honor** to the Father — *because* the Father has so willed it. The honor given to the Son flows to the Father (Phil 2:11 — *"to the glory of God the Father"*).

---

## **3.5 — Authority to Give the Spirit**

### **John 7:37-39, 1599 Geneva:**

> … If any man thirst, let him come unto me, and drink. He that believeth in me, as saith the Scripture, out of his belly shall flow rivers of water of life. ... (This spake he of the Spirit which they that believed in him, should receive … — **John 7:37-39, 1599 Geneva**

### **Acts 2:33, 1599 Geneva:**

> Since then that he by the right hand of God hath been exalted, and hath received of his Father the promise of the holy Ghost, he hath shed forth this which ye now see and hear. — **Acts 2:33, 1599 Geneva**

---

### Observation

Peter's Pentecost sermon explicitly states that Jesus **received** the promise of the Holy Spirit **from the Father** and then **poured it forth.** The Son is both Recipient (from the Father) and Giver (to His people). The pattern is unbroken: the Father is the Source; the Son is the channel.
