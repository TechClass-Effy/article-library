## **THE FOURTH APPEARANCE — Genesis 32: Wrestling at Peniel**

> And Iakob *(Jacob)* was left alone, and a man was wrestling with him until morning. … Then he said to him, “Your name shall no longer be called Iakob *(Jacob)*, but Israel shall be your name, because you have prevailed with a god, and with humans you are powerful.” … And Iakob *(Jacob)* called the name of that place Divine-form *(Peniel, “the face of God”)*, “For I have seen a god face to face, and my life has been preserved.” — **Genesis 32:24-30, LXX (NETS; the Greek editions number these verses 25-31)**

### Observation of the highest weight

The One who wrestles is called **ἄνθρωπος** — *a man.* He is **physically embodied**, wrestling all night. Yet:

- He has authority to **rename** Jacob to **Israel** (only God renames covenant figures).

- He declares Jacob has **striven with God** (**μετὰ Θεοῦ**).

- Jacob identifies Him as **Θεός** — *"I have seen God face to face."*

Yet this God is in the form of a **Man**. Here is a pre-incarnate visible manifestation of a Person who is both *with God* (whom Jacob strove with) and *appears as Man* — a foreshadowing of the Incarnation itself.

---

### The Prophet Hosea confirms — Hosea 12:3-4 (LXX 12:4-5):

> In the womb he kicked his brother with the heel and in his struggles prevailed against God. And he prevailed with an angel and predominated *(prevailed, overpowered)* …  — **Hosea 12:3-4, LXX (NETS; the Greek editions number these verses 4-5)**

**The Prophet's testimony is the second witness:** In one breath, Jacob's adversary is called **Θεός** *(God)* and **ἄγγελος** *(Angel/Messenger).* **Hosea, under inspiration, identifies the Messenger as God.** The two designations refer to the same Person.

## **THE FIFTH APPEARANCE — Exodus 3: The Burning Bush**

> Now an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a fire of flame out of the bush, … Now when the Lord saw that he was drawing near to see, the Lord called him from the bush, saying, “Moyses *(Moses)*, Moyses.” And he said, “What is it?” … And he said to him, “I am the God of your father, God of Abraam *(Abraham)* and God of Isaak *(Isaac)* and God of Iakob *(Jacob)*.” …  — **Exodus 3:2-6, LXX (NETS)**

### Observation of the highest order

The text moves seamlessly between three designations:

- **ἄγγελος Κυρίου** *(the Messenger of the LORD)* — v. 2

- **Κύριος** *(the LORD)* — v. 4

- **ὁ Θεός** *(God)* — v. 4, 6

**The Messenger, the LORD, and God are presented as one and the same speaker** — yet the text begins by calling Him "the Messenger of the LORD," not "the LORD" simpliciter. Once again: **He speaks as God, is identified as God, yet is named the Messenger sent by God.**

---

### **Exodus 3:14 (LXX) — The "I AM" Declaration:**

> And God said to Moyses *(Moses)*, “I am The One Who Is.” And he said, “Thus shall you say to the sons of Israel, ‘The One Who Is has sent me to you.’” — **Exodus 3:14, LXX (NETS)**

### Observation

The Messenger declares Himself: **Ἐγώ εἰμι ὁ ὤν** — *"I AM the One Who Is."* This is the divine Name itself.

Now hear Jesus, in the New Testament, in John 8:58 (Greek):

*"εἶπεν αὐτοῖς Ἰησοῦς· Ἀμὴν ἀμὴν λέγω ὑμῖν, πρὶν Ἀβραὰμ γενέσθαι ἐγὼ εἰμί."*

> Jesus said unto them, Verily, verily I say unto you, before Abraham was, I am. — **John 8:58, 1599 Geneva**

The Jews understood this and sought to stone Him for blasphemy. **Jesus claims the very designation the Messenger spoke at the burning bush.** This is a strong witness — though not yet decisive — that the Messenger of the LORD is the same Person who later was named Jesus.
