## **Part 2 — Jesus' Self-Identifications: the "I AM" Sayings, the Son of Man, the Son of God, and the Titles He Took**

# **PART 2 — JESUS' SELF-IDENTIFICATIONS**

We must take this seriously and carefully, for here we hear from the Person Himself.

## **2.1 — The "I AM" Sayings of John**

Jesus uses **ἐγώ εἰμι** (*I am*) repeatedly in John's Gospel, sometimes with a predicate, sometimes absolutely.

### **With a predicate** (revealing His functions):

- **John 6:35:** *ἐγώ εἰμι ὁ ἄρτος τῆς ζωῆς* *(I am the bread of life)*
- **John 8:12:** *ἐγώ εἰμι τὸ φῶς τοῦ κόσμου* *(I am the light of the world)*
- **John 10:7, 9:** *ἐγώ εἰμι ἡ θύρα* *(I am the door)*
- **John 10:11, 14:** *ἐγώ εἰμι ὁ ποιμὴν ὁ καλός* *(I am the good shepherd)*
- **John 11:25:** *ἐγώ εἰμι ἡ ἀνάστασις καὶ ἡ ζωή* *(I am the resurrection and the life)*
- **John 14:6:** *ἐγώ εἰμι ἡ ὁδὸς καὶ ἡ ἀλήθεια καὶ ἡ ζωή* *(I am the way, the truth, and the life)*
- **John 15:1, 5:** *ἐγώ εἰμι ἡ ἄμπελος ἡ ἀληθινή* *(I am the true vine)*

### **The predicate sayings, 1599 Geneva:**

> And Jesus said unto them, I am that bread of life: he that cometh to me, shall not hunger, and he that believeth in me, shall never thirst. — **John 6:35, 1599 Geneva**

> Then spake Jesus again unto them, saying, I am that light of the world: he that followeth me, shall not walke in darkenes, but shall have that light of life. — **John 8:12, 1599 Geneva**

> Then said Jesus unto them again, Verily, verily I say unto you, I am that door of the sheep. — **John 10:7, 1599 Geneva**

> I am that door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved, and shall go in, and go out, and find pasture. — **John 10:9, 1599 Geneva**

> I am that good shepherd: that good shepherd giveth his life for his sheep. — **John 10:11, 1599 Geneva**

> I am that good shepherd, and knowe mine, and am knowen of mine. — **John 10:14, 1599 Geneva**

> Jesus said unto her, I am the resurrection and the life: he that believeth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he live. — **John 11:25, 1599 Geneva**

> Jesus said unto him, I am that Way, and that Truth, and that Life. No man cometh unto the Father, but by me. — **John 14:6, 1599 Geneva**

> I am that true vine, and my Father is that husband man *(the vine-dresser)*. — **John 15:1, 1599 Geneva**

> I am that vine: ye are the branches: he that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruite: for without me can ye do nothing. — **John 15:5, 1599 Geneva**

### **Absolute "I AM" (without predicate):**

### **John 8:24, 1599 Geneva:**

> I said therefore unto you, That ye shall die in your sins: for except ye believe, that I am he, ye shall die in your sins. — **John 8:24, 1599 Geneva**

### **John 8:58** (already cited): *"Before Abraham was, I am."*

### **John 13:19, 1599 Geneva:**

> From henceforth tell I you before it come, that when it is come to pass, ye might believe that I am he. — **John 13:19, 1599 Geneva**

### **John 18:5-6, 1599 Geneva:**

> … Jesus said unto them, I am he. ... As soon then as he had said unto them, I am he, they went away backwards, and fell to the ground. — **John 18:5-6, 1599 Geneva**

---

### Observation

The absolute **ἐγώ εἰμι** sayings echo the LXX of Isaiah 41:4, 43:10, 13, 25, 46:4, 48:12 — where the LORD repeatedly says **ἐγώ εἰμι**. Jesus' use of this formulation, especially with the response of the Jews wanting to stone Him for blasphemy (John 8:59), indicates He is taking up that voice.

**Honest assessment:** The absolute **ἐγώ εἰμι** sayings are *strong* witnesses to Jesus' divine self-disclosure — bearing the Name and voice of the LORD. They are not, however, declarations *"I am the LORD"* or *"I am the Father."* They are claims to bear the LORD's self-identifying speech — consistent with His being the One in whom **the Name dwells** (cf. Ex 23:21; John 17:11).

## **2.2 — Son of Man**

This is Jesus' **most frequent self-designation** — used over 80 times in the Gospels. Its background is **Daniel 7:13-14**.

### **Daniel 7:13-14, NETS (Old Greek):**

> I was watching in the night visions, and lo, as it were a son of man was coming upon the clouds of heaven. And he came as far as the ancient of days, and the attendants were present with him. And royal authority was given to him, and all the nations of the earth according to posterity, and all honor was serving him. And his authority is an everlasting authority, which shall never be removed— and his kingship, which will never perish. — **Daniel 7:13-14, NETS (Old Greek)**

*NETS’s Daniel carries Old Greek and Theodotion in parallel columns; the draft cites the LXX voice. Theodotion reads “…coming with the clouds of heaven… And he came as far as the ancient of days and was presented to him. And to him was given the dominion and the honor and the kingship…” and the kingdom “will not be destroyed” *(Theodotion, the parallel column)*. The draft’s LXX Greek also carries καὶ ὡς παλαιὸς ἡμερῶν παρῆν — “and, as Ancient of days, he was present” — which the Old Greek print renders “And he came as far as the ancient of days.”*

### **Cross-witnesses — Jesus' direct citations:**

### **Matthew 26:63-64, 1599 Geneva — Jesus’ confession before the high priest:**

> … the chief Priest answered, and said to him, I charge thee swear unto us by the living God, to tell us, If thou be that Christ the Son of God, or no. ... Jesus said to him, Thou hast said it: nevertheless I say unto you, hereafter shall ye see the Son of man, sitting at the right hand of the power of God, and come in the clouds of the heaven. — **Matthew 26:63-64, 1599 Geneva**

---

### Observation

**Jesus' confession before the high priest combines:**
- **Psalm 110:1** (*"sitting at the right hand of power"*)
- **Daniel 7:13** (*"coming on the clouds of heaven"*)

This is **Jesus' own claim of identity**: He is the **Son of Man** of Daniel 7, who will be enthroned and given universal dominion by the **Ancient of Days** (the Father). His title **Son of Man** is therefore not merely a humble human designation — it is a claim to the **prophesied Heavenly Figure** who will receive eternal dominion from the Father.

This is also the **second witness alongside Psalm 110** for the Son's enthronement at the Father's right hand.

**An important note for our study:** In Daniel 7, the **Son of Man** is *brought before* the **Ancient of Days** — He is *not* the Ancient of Days. He is a *distinct figure* who *receives* dominion *from* the Ancient of Days. This perfectly preserves our foundation: the Father is the One God on the throne; the Son receives all authority from Him.

## **2.3 — Son of God / The Son**

Jesus often refers to Himself as **the Son** in relation to **the Father**. Key examples:

### **Matthew 11:27, 1599 Geneva:**

> All things are given unto me of my Father: and no man knoweth the Son, but ye Father: neither knoweth any man ye Father, but the Son, and he to whom ye Son will reueile him. — **Matthew 11:27, 1599 Geneva**

### **Mark 13:32, 1599 Geneva:**

> But of that day and hour knoweth no man, no, not the Angels which are in heaven, neither the Son himself, but the Father. — **Mark 13:32, 1599 Geneva**

---

### Observation of considerable importance for our study

**Mark 13:32 is a critical text.** Jesus explicitly says:
- The **Son does not know** the day or hour.
- The **Father alone** knows.

This is Jesus' **own testimony** that the Father has knowledge the Son does not have. **This is consistent with our entire foundation:** the Father is the One supreme God, source of all things; the Son is the Sent One who operates in dependence and submission. He does not know what the Father has kept in His own authority (cf. Acts 1:7).

This text alone is decisive against any reading that flattens the Son into being "of one substance" with the Father in such a way that He has all the Father's knowledge. **Per maxim #1, let God be true** — and Jesus Himself says **the Son does not know what the Father knows**.
