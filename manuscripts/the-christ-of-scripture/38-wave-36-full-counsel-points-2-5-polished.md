## **POINT 2 — DEAD WITH CHRIST**

### **Romans 6:8:**

### **1599 Geneva:**
*"Wherefore, if we be dead with Christ, we believe that we shall live also with him,"*

### **2 Timothy 2:11:**

### **1599 Geneva:**
*"It is a true saying, For if we be dead together with him, we also shall live together with him."*

### **Colossians 2:20; 3:3:**

### **1599 Geneva:**
*"Wherefore if ye be dead with Christ from the ordinances of the world..."*
*"For ye are dead, and your life is hid with Christ in God."*

---

### Observation

The believer has **died with Christ.** This is parallel to the co-crucifixion but extends the truth: not only was the old man crucified, but the believer **has died** — and this death is the basis for:
- Freedom from sin's dominion (Rom 6).
- Freedom from the elements/principles of the world (Col 2:20).
- A new hidden life with Christ in God (Col 3:3).

**The believer's life is hidden with Christ in God** — a profound expression of identification: our true life is not visible to the world, because it is hidden in the Son, who is hidden in the Father.

---

## **POINT 3 — BURIED WITH CHRIST**

### **Romans 6:4:**

### **1599 Geneva:**
*"We are buried then with him by baptism into his death, that like as Christ was raised up from the dead to the glory of the Father, so we also should walk in newness of life."*

### **Colossians 2:12:**

### **1599 Geneva:**
*"In that ye are buried with him through baptism, in whom ye are also raised up together through the faith of the operation of God, which raised him from the dead."*

---

### Observation

The believer has been **buried with Christ.** Baptism (immersion) symbolically depicts this burial. **Two witnesses (Rom 6 and Col 2) establish:** baptism is the outward sign of the believer's inward identification with Christ in His death, burial, and resurrection.

The burial seals the death. What is buried cannot be brought back to its old life — only resurrection can bring it forth, and resurrection brings forth something new.

---

## **POINT 4 — RAISED WITH CHRIST**

### **Romans 6:4-5:**

### **1599 Geneva:**
*"...that like as Christ was raised up from the dead to the glory of the Father, so we also should walk in newness of life. For if we be planted with him to the similitude of his death, even so shall we be to the similitude of his resurrection,"*

### **Colossians 2:12 (already cited):** *"...in whom ye are also raised up together..."*

### **Colossians 3:1:**

### **1599 Geneva:**
*"If ye then be risen with Christ, seek those things which are above, where Christ sitteth at the right hand of God."*

### **Ephesians 2:5-6:**

### **1599 Geneva:**
*"Even when we were dead by sins, hath quickened us together in Christ, by whose grace ye are saved, And hath raised us up together..."*

---

### Observation

The believer has been **raised with Christ.** This is a present spiritual reality (not merely future bodily resurrection). **Three witnesses (Rom 6, Col 2-3, Eph 2) establish:** by the same power that raised Christ from the dead, the Father has raised us up *with* Christ.

This means:
- **We have new life now** — the resurrection life of the Son (Rom 6:4).
- **Our orientation is now upward** — toward where Christ is (Col 3:1).
- **The Father has co-resurrected us with His Son** (Eph 2:6) — by the same power.

---

## **POINT 5 — SEATED WITH CHRIST AT THE FATHER'S RIGHT HAND**

### **Ephesians 2:6:**

### **1599 Geneva:**
*"...And hath raised us up together, and made us sit together in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus,"*

### **Colossians 3:1, 3** — already cited; the believer's life is hidden with Christ in God, where Christ sits at the right hand.

---

### Observation of remarkable importance

**συνεκάθισεν ἐν τοῖς ἐπουρανίοις** — *"He has made [us] sit together in the heavenly places."*

The Father has not only raised us with Christ; **He has seated us with Christ in the heavenly places.** Where Christ sits — at the Father's right hand — **we sit with Him.**

This is breathtaking. The very session of the Son at the Father's right hand — which we examined under Part A as the central reality of His present ministry — **includes us by identification.** We are not merely beneficiaries of His enthronement; we are **co-seated with Him.**

This corresponds with:
- **Revelation 3:21** — *"To him that overcometh will I grant to sit with me in my throne, even as I also overcame, and am set down with my Father in his throne."*
- **2 Timothy 2:12** — *"if we suffer, we shall also reign with him."*
- **Romans 8:17** — *"and if children, then heirs; heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ; if so be that we suffer with him, that we may be also glorified together."*

**The believer's seat is with the Son, at the Father's right hand.** Not in our own merit, but **in Christ Jesus** (ἐν Χριστῷ Ἰησοῦ — the controlling phrase).
