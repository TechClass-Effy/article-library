# **PART 10 — STEWARDSHIP**

The believer recognizes that **nothing is truly his own** — all has been entrusted by the Father for His purposes.

## **The Foundation**

### **1 Corinthians 4:7**

*"For who separateth thee? and what hast thou, that thou hast not received? if thou hast received it, why rejoicest thou, as though thou hadst not received it?"*

The believer has received everything he has. **He is not an owner but a steward.**

---

## **Time Stewardship**

### **Ephesians 5:15-17**

*"Take heed therefore that ye walk circumspectly, not as fools, but as wise, Redeeming the season: for the days are evil. Wherefore, be ye not unwise, but understand what the will of the Lord is."*

The believer is to **redeem the time** (ἐξαγοραζόμενοι τὸν καιρόν — *"buying up the opportunity"*). Time is finite and irretrievable; the believer uses it wisely for the Father's purposes.

---

## **Material Stewardship**

### **1 Timothy 6:17-19**

*"Charge them that are rich in this world, that they be not high-minded, and that they trust not in uncertain riches, but in the living God, (which giveth us abundantly, all things to enjoy) That they do good, and be rich in good works, and ready to distribute, and communicate *(share)*, Laying up in store for themselves a good foundation against the time to come, that they may obtain eternal life."*

The believer with material resources:
- Does not put trust in riches.
- Does good with his resources.
- Is ready to distribute.
- Is generous, willing to share.

### **2 Corinthians 9:6-8**

*"This yet remember, that he which soweth sparingly, shall reap also sparingly, and he that soweth liberally, shall reap also liberally. As every man wisheth in his heart, so let him give, not grudgingly, or of necessity: for God loveth a cheerful giver. And God is able to make all grace to abound toward you, that ye always having all sufficiency in all things, may abound in every good work,"*

Giving is to be:
- **Decided in advance** (as one purposes in his heart).
- **Cheerful** (not grudging).
- **Generous** (he who sows sparingly reaps sparingly).
- **Trusting** (God is able to make all grace abound).

---

## **Stewardship of Gifts**

### **1 Peter 4:10**

*"Let every man as he hath received the gift, minister the same one to another, as good disposers *(stewards)* of the manifold grace of God."*

Every believer has received gifts. Each is to **minister the gift to others** as a steward of God's manifold grace.

---

## **Practical Implications**

**1. Use your time intentionally.** Identify priorities — the Father's purposes, family, ministry, work, rest. Refuse time-wasters that have no eternal value.

**2. Give generously and cheerfully.** Set aside a regular portion of income for the Lord's work. Be ready to give beyond when needs arise.

**3. Use your gifts in service to the Body.** Discover what you have been given and pour it out.

**4. Live simply.** Resist the world's pressure toward consumerism. *"Having food and raiment, let us be therewith content"* (1 Tim 6:8 Geneva).

**5. Plan for the future faithfully** — but with open hands, knowing that all is the Father's.

**6. Hold possessions loosely.** They are the Father's, not yours. If He calls you to release them, release them.

**7. Be hospitable.** Open your home, your table, your resources to those who need them.

# **GRAND SYNTHESIS — THE ARCHITECTURE OF THE FAITHFUL CHRISTIAN LIFE**

Let me bring together the ten disciplines we have surveyed into a coherent picture of the Christian life.

## **The Daily Pattern**

**Morning:**
- Prayer (Adoration, Kingdom, Surrender, Provision, Confession, Deliverance).
- The Word (reading, meditation, perhaps memorization).
- The reckoning of identity in Christ.
- Surrender of the day to the Father.

**Throughout the Day:**
- Continuous prayer (brief turning of the heart).
- Walking by the Spirit.
- Stewarding time, gifts, resources.
- Watchfulness against temptation.
- Witness through life and word as opportunities arise.
- Service to others.
- Confession of sin when convicted.

**Evening:**
- Review of the day before the Lord.
- Confession of known sin.
- Thanksgiving for mercies received.
- Commitment to faithfulness tomorrow.

## **The Weekly Pattern**

- Gathering with the saints.
- Hearing of the Word preached.
- The Lord's Supper (where observed weekly).
- Fellowship and mutual encouragement.
- Use of gifts to build up the Body.

## **The Ongoing Pattern**

- Deepening relationships within the Body.
- Bearing one another's burdens.
- Intercession for the saints, the lost, the world.
- Faithful witness in the spheres of life.
- Patient endurance under trial.
- Progressive sanctification — being conformed to the Son's image.
- Looking for the blessed hope.

## **The Whole-Life Pattern**

- Body presented as a living sacrifice.
- Mind renewed by the Word.
- Affections set on things above.
- Members yielded as weapons of righteousness.
- Words and deeds done in the Name of the Lord Jesus.
- All accompanied by thanksgiving to the Father through Him.

---

# **A FINAL OBSERVATION**

Brother, what we have surveyed is **the texture of a faithful Christian life** — the disciplines and responses that flow from identification with Christ.

None of these are means of earning salvation; salvation is the Father's free gift in His Son. These are the **expressions** of a salvation already received, the **means** by which the Spirit's work in us is cooperated with, the **practices** in which the indicative-imperative pattern is lived out.

The modern believer who walks in these disciplines is:
- **In daily communion** with the Father through prayer.
- **Being renewed** in the mind through the Word.
- **Worshipping** the Father in Spirit and truth.
- **Connected** to the Body of Christ in mutual love and ministry.
- **Remembering** the Lord at His Table.
- **Watchful** against the adversary and temptation.
- **Honest** about sin and quick to confess.
- **Trusting** the Father in every trial.
- **Bearing witness** to the Son in word and life.
- **Faithful** in stewardship of all that has been entrusted.

This is not a burden of laws. **It is the joyful response of one who has been loved with everlasting love, redeemed by precious blood, indwelt by the Spirit of God, and destined for the inheritance of the saints in light.**

The believer walks in these disciplines not to **become** a child of God, but because **he is** a child of God. Not to **earn** the Father's favor, but because **he has** the Father's favor in the Beloved. Not to **achieve** glory, but because **glory is laid up** for him in Christ Jesus.

This is the Christian life — and to the Father, through the Son, by the Spirit, it is the highest and happiest way for any human being to live in this world before the appearing of the Son.
