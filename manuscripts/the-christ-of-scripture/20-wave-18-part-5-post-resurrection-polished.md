# **PART 5 — POST-RESURRECTION ACTS**

## **5.1 — Authentication of the Resurrection**

The risen Jesus:
- Appears to Mary Magdalene (John 20:11-18).
- Appears to the disciples and shows His wounds (Luke 24:36-43; John 20:19-29).
- Eats with His disciples (Luke 24:42-43; Acts 10:41) — proving He is bodily, not a phantom.
- Appears to 500 brethren at once (1 Cor 15:6).
- Opens their understanding of the Scriptures (Luke 24:25-27, 44-47).

## **5.2 — The Great Commission**

### **Matthew 28:18-20:**

> ... All power is given unto me, in heaven, and in earth. Go therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the Name of the Father, and the Son, and the holy Ghost, Teaching them to observe all things, whatsoever I have commanded you: and lo, I am with you alway, until the end of the world, Amen. — **Matthew 28:18-20, 1599 Geneva**

---

### Observation

**(1)** **Ἐδόθη μοι πᾶσα ἐξουσία** — *"All authority **has been given** to Me."* Even after resurrection, the Son receives His authority **from** the Father. The passive verb is decisive.

**(2)** The baptismal formula names **the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit** — though notice carefully that the **Name** (singular: **εἰς τὸ ὄνομα**) is one Name, not three. This text deserves more careful examination later in our study, but at minimum it establishes that the Father, the Son, and the Spirit are jointly involved in the disciple-making mission — though Jesus does *not* here teach a metaphysical doctrine of "three persons in one substance." He commands a baptism in a singular Name shared by the three.

We should note, however, that the historical practice of the apostles in Acts is to baptize **"in the Name of Jesus Christ"** (Acts 2:38; 8:16; 10:48; 19:5). This warrants careful examination at the appropriate place — perhaps when we treat the Apocalypse's view of "the Name."

## **5.3 — Ascension and Promise of Return**

### **Acts 1:9-11:**

> ... And when he had spoken these things, while they beheld, he was taken up: for a cloud took him up out of their sight. ... Which also said, Ye men of Galilee, why stand ye gazing into heaven? This Jesus which is taken up from you into heaven, shall so come, as ye have seen him go into heaven. — **Acts 1:9-11, 1599 Geneva**

---

### Observation

The ascension fulfills the *"sit at My right hand"* of Psalm 110:1 (per Acts 2:34-35). The angelic promise — *"shall so come as ye have seen Him go"* — establishes the framework for our later examination of His return.

# **GRAND SYNTHESIS — What His Ministry Establishes**

Through the acts and words of His ministry, the following are established by the most extensive direct evidence:

### **About His Person:**

1. **He is the prophesied Servant of the LORD** — by His own citation of Isaiah 61 (Luke 4:17-21).
2. **He is the Son of Man of Daniel 7** — by His own claim before the high priest (Matt 26:64).
3. **He is the Christ, the Son of God** — by His own confession (Matt 26:64; John 4:25-26).
4. **He bears the LORD's voice** — the **ἐγώ εἰμι** sayings.
5. **He is greater than Solomon, Jonah, and the temple** — by His own testimony.
6. **He is the embodiment of the LORD's prophesied works** — His miracles fulfill Isaiah 35, 53, 61.

### **About His Authority:**

7. **He forgives sins** — exercised on earth as the Son of Man.
8. **He commands nature.**
9. **He raises the dead.**
10. **He has been given all judgment.**
11. **He gives the Holy Spirit** — having received it from the Father.
12. **He has been given all authority** — explicitly **given**, not innately possessed apart from the Father.

### **About His Relation to the Father (His Own Words):**

13. **He can do nothing of Himself** — only what He sees the Father doing.
14. **He does not seek His own will** — but the Father's.
15. **He speaks what the Father has taught Him.**
16. **The Father is greater than Him.**
17. **The Father alone is the only true God; the Son is the Sent One.**
18. **The Son does not know what the Father knows** (the day and hour).

### **About His Mission:**

19. **He came to fulfill, not destroy** the Law and Prophets (Matt 5:17).
20. **He came not to be served, but to serve, and to give His life a ransom for many** (Mark 10:45 — echoing Isa 53).
21. **He came to seek and save the lost** (Luke 19:10).
22. **He came that they might have life, and have it abundantly** (John 10:10).

### **Post-Resurrection:**

23. **He is risen bodily** — bearing His pierced hands and feet (fulfilling Ps 22:16).
24. **He receives all authority from the Father** after resurrection.
25. **He commissions worldwide proclamation.**
26. **He ascends and is enthroned** at the Father's right hand (fulfilling Ps 110:1).
27. **He will return in like manner** as He ascended.
