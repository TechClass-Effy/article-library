# **PASSAGE A — JOHN 10:30**

## **John 10:30 — Geneva 1599:**

*"I and my Father are one."*

---

## **Context (John 10:22-39)**

Jesus is at the feast of Dedication in Jerusalem, walking in Solomon's porch. The Jews demand: *"How long dost thou make us doubt? If thou be the Christ, tell us plainly."* (v. 24).

Jesus answers with a discourse about His sheep — those the Father has given Him:

### **John 10:27-30 — Geneva 1599:**

*"My sheep hear my voice... And I give unto them eternal life, and they shall never perish, neither shall any pluck them out of mine hand. My Father which gave them me, is greater then *(than)* all, and none is able to take them out of my Fathers hand. I and my Father are one."*

---

## **Careful Greek Examination**

### **Note carefully the immediate context:**

**v. 29 — "My Father which gave them me, is greater than all"** — Jesus explicitly acknowledges the Father as **greater** and as the One who **gave** the sheep to Him. This is the same pattern we have established throughout: the Father is the Source; the Son receives.

### **The word "ἕν" — critical grammatical observation**

**ἕν** is the neuter of the numeral "one." Not the masculine **εἷς** (which would signify identity of person). The neuter **ἕν** signifies **oneness in a thing, a purpose, an operation** — not identity of Person.

**This is decisive.** If Jesus had wanted to claim identity of Person with the Father, He would have used **εἷς**. His deliberate use of **ἕν** points to a different kind of oneness.

---

## **Under Our Framework**

### **The Name-Authority Reading**

Under your two insights, Jesus' claim resolves as follows:

**The Son and the Father are ONE (ἕν)** in:
- **Purpose** — no sheep can be plucked from either hand, because both act with the same protective will.
- **Operation** — what the Father gives, the Son secures.
- **Authority** — the Son bears the Father's Name and acts with the Father's authority.
- **Spirit-nature** — the Son shares the Father's Spirit-nature as the Only-Begotten.

**But not identity of Person.** The very context distinguishes them:
- *"My Father gave them me"* — two distinct actors.
- *"My Father which is greater than all"* — the Father's supremacy explicitly maintained.
- *"MY hand... MY Father's hand"* — two distinct hands.

### **Confirmation from John 17**

Jesus prays that His disciples may be **ἓν** — one — **as We are one:**

### **John 17:11 — Geneva 1599:**

*"...that they may be one, as we are."*

### **John 17:21-22 — Geneva 1599:**

*"That they all may be one, as thou, O Father, art in me, and I in thee: even that they may be also one in us... that they may be one, as we are one,"*

---

### Observation of decisive importance

**Jesus explicitly uses the same word ἕν to describe both:**
- His oneness with the Father.
- The oneness the disciples are to have with the Father and Son.

**Since the disciples' oneness with the Father and Son cannot be metaphysical identity of Person** (else they would become God), **neither is Jesus' oneness with the Father metaphysical identity of Person.**

**The oneness is a real, living, mutually indwelling union** — of purpose, will, operation, and Spirit-communion — but **not identity of Person.**

**This resolves John 10:30 decisively under our maxims.**

---

## **The Jews' Reaction (John 10:31-33)**

### **John 10:31-33 — Geneva 1599:**

*"...Then the Jews again took up stones, to stone him... For blasphemy, and that thou being a man, makest thyself God."*

---

### Jesus' Response (John 10:34-36):

### **John 10:34-36 — Geneva 1599:**

*"...Is it not written in your Law, I said, ye are gods? If he called them gods, unto whom the word of God was given, and the Scripture cannot be broken, Say ye of him, whom the Father hath sanctified, and sent into the world, Thou blasphemest, because I said, I am the Son of God?"*

---

### Observation of critical importance

**Jesus' own response is decisive for our interpretation.**

**The Jews accused Him of "making Himself God"** — of claiming identical personal deity with the Father.

**Jesus does NOT confirm this accusation.** He does NOT say: *"Yes, I am God in the sense you understand."*

Instead, Jesus cites **Psalm 82:6 LXX** — where mere human judges are called *"gods"* because the word/authority of God came upon them. Jesus argues from lesser to greater:
- If mere humans could be called "gods" because God's word came to them,
- How much more the One whom **the Father sanctified and sent** — bearing the Father's own Name and authority?

**Jesus' self-designation is: "Son of God" — not "God the Son."** He is the Sent One, sanctified by the Father, bearing the Father's Name.

**His claim is:**
- Oneness (ἕν) with the Father — in purpose, will, operation, and shared Spirit-nature.
- The Son of God — bearing the Father's Name given to Him.
- Sent by the Father into the world.

**His claim is NOT:**
- Identity of Person with the Father.
- Being "God" in the sense the Jews accused Him of.

**Under our maxims, John 10:30 is fully coherent with our framework:**

**The Son, begotten of the Father in Spirit, bearing the Father's Name, sharing His Spirit-nature, sent to accomplish the Father's will, indwelt by the Father — is ONE with the Father in every functional and relational sense that matters. But He is not identical Person with the Father.**
