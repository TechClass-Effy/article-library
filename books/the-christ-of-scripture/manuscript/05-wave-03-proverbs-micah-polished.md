## **A QUESTION ARISING — Was the Son Brought Forth Before the World Was?**

Psalm 109:3 LXX has opened the question of the Son's **pre-temporal begetting** — *"from the womb, before the morning star."* Two passages must now be examined to see whether and how Scripture testifies to the Son's existence *before* His historical birth in Bethlehem and His being declared Son in power at the resurrection: **Proverbs 8**, where **Wisdom** speaks in the first person, and **Micah 5:2** — *"whose goings forth have been from of old."*

We will proceed carefully — letting the text speak, and not retrojecting later "eternal generation" formulations.

---

## **PROVERBS 8 — Wisdom Brought Forth Before the Foundation of the World**

The chapter is spoken in the **first person** by **Σοφία** — *Wisdom* — personified throughout Proverbs 1-9. The question we must ask: **Is this Wisdom the Son? And if so, what does the text say about His origin?**

We will not assume the identification; we will let Scripture establish it by two witnesses.

---

### **Proverbs 8:22-25 (LXX):**

> The Lord created me as the beginning of his ways, for the sake of his works. Before the present age he founded me, in the beginning. Before he made the earth and before he made the depths, before he brought forth the springs of the waters, before the mountains were established and before all the hills, he begets me. — **Proverbs 8:22-25, LXX (NETS)**

---

### Observations of decisive importance

**(1)** The LXX reads **Κύριος ἔκτισέν με** — *"The Lord created/founded me."* The verb is **κτίζω**. The Geneva follows the Hebrew *qanah*, which can mean *possess, acquire, or beget.* This is the verse over which the great Arian controversy raged in the 4th century — and it is one of the texts the post-Nicene party labored most strenuously to suppress or reinterpret, precisely because the LXX (which Jesus and the Apostles used — per our maxims) reads **ἔκτισέν με**.

**Let God be true.** The LXX says: **The Lord brought-forth/founded Wisdom as the beginning (ἀρχήν) of His ways, for His works.**

**(2)** **πρὸ τοῦ αἰῶνος ἐθεμελίωσέν με ἐν ἀρχῇ** — *"Before the age He established me in the beginning."* Wisdom is founded by the Lord **before time itself**, before the **αἰών**.

**(3)** **πρὸ δὲ πάντων βουνῶν γεννᾷ με** — *"Before all hills He BEGETS me."* The verb **γεννάω** — *to beget* — is the same root as **γεγέννηκά σε** of Psalm 2:7 and **ἐγέννησά σε** of Psalm 109:3 LXX. **Three witnesses now agree on the same vocabulary**: the Son is **begotten** of the Father.

---

### **Proverbs 8:27, 30 (LXX):**

> When he prepared the sky *(the heavens)*, I was present with him, and when he marked out his own throne on the winds. … I was beside him, fitting together; it is I who was the one in whom he took delight. And each day I was glad in his presence at every moment, — **Proverbs 8:27, 30, LXX (NETS)**

---

### Observations

**(1)** **συμπαρήμην αὐτῷ** — *"I was present alongside Him."* Wisdom is **with** the Lord at the creation. This is precisely the language John 1:1 will echo: **ὁ Λόγος ἦν πρὸς τὸν Θεόν** — *"the Word was with God."*

**(2)** **ἤμην παρ᾽ αὐτῷ ἁρμόζουσα** — *"I was beside Him, fitting [things] together / harmonizing."* The Son, as Wisdom, is the Father's *Agent of arrangement* in creation. This now begins to reconcile Isaiah 44:24 ("I stretched out the heavens **alone**") with 1 Cor 8:6 ("through whom are all things"): **the Father alone is the Source; the Son is His Wisdom by whom He fits the works together.** The Father does it; He does it *by* His Wisdom — His Wisdom is not a second God, but His own Begotten One acting as His instrument.

**(3)** **καθ᾽ ἡμέραν δὲ εὐφραινόμην ἐν προσώπῳ αὐτοῦ** — *"daily I was rejoicing in His face/presence."* The mutual delight between the Father and His Begotten One **before** the world was made.

---

### Is "Wisdom" in Proverbs 8 the Son? — The two witnesses:

**Witness #1 — 1 Corinthians 1:24, 30 (Geneva):**

> But unto them which are called, both of the Jews and Grecians, we preach Christ, the power of God, and the wisdom of God. … But ye are of him in Christ Jesus, who of God is made unto us wisdom and righteousness, and sanctification, and redemption, — **1 Corinthians 1:24, 30, 1599 Geneva**

Paul explicitly identifies Christ as **Θεοῦ σοφίαν** — *the Wisdom of God* — and says He was **made** (**ἐγενήθη**) Wisdom **to us** **ἀπὸ Θεοῦ** — *from God.* The Wisdom of Proverbs 8 is fulfilled in the Person of Christ.

**Witness #2 — Colossians 1:15-17 (Geneva):**

> Who is the image of the invisible God, the first begotten of every creature. For by him were all things created, which are in heaven, and which are in earth, things visible and invisible: whether they be Thrones or Dominions, or Principalities, or Powers, all things were created by him, and for him, And he is before all things, and in him all things consist. — **Colossians 1:15-17, 1599 Geneva**

**πρωτότοκος πάσης κτίσεως** — *"firstborn of all creation"* — corresponds precisely to **ἀρχὴν ὁδῶν αὐτοῦ** of Proverbs 8:22 LXX — the Beginning of God's ways. And note the verb in both texts: **κτίζω** — the very word used of Wisdom in Prov 8:22 and of all things being created **in** Christ in Col 1:16.

**Two witnesses established:** Proverbs 8 is speaking of the Son in His pre-temporal relation to the Father.

---

### What Proverbs 8 establishes about the Son:

1. He is **brought forth / founded** by the Lord as **the beginning of His ways** (v. 22).
2. He is **begotten** before the creation of the earth, depths, mountains (vv. 24-25).
3. He is **with** the Father **at** the work of creation, serving as the Father's Wisdom by which all things are fitted together (vv. 27-30).
4. He is the Father's **delight** before the world is made (v. 30).
5. **He is not the Father; He is brought forth from the Father, distinct from Him, beside Him.**

---

### A doctrinal observation per our maxims

Notice carefully what Proverbs 8 does **not** say:

- It does **not** say Wisdom is co-eternal in the sense of having no beginning. It explicitly says she was **founded / begotten** by the Lord.
- It does **not** say Wisdom is *another God*. She is the Lord's Wisdom — belonging to Him, brought forth from Him.
- It does **not** violate Isaiah 44:24. The Lord stretches out the heavens *alone* — by His own Wisdom, which is His. The Wisdom acting is *His*, not a separate Deity.

This perfectly preserves the Shema and Isaiah 44-46 while honestly testifying to the Son's pre-temporal begetting. **The Son has an origin in the Father; the Father has no origin.** This is the consistent biblical witness, and it is the testimony of the LXX our Lord used.

---

## **MICAH 5:2 — The Goings Forth From of Old**

### **Micah 5:2 (LXX):**

> And you, O Bethleem *(Bethlehem)*, house of Ephratha *(Ephrathah)*, are very few in number to be among the thousands of Ioudas *(Judah)*; one from you shall come forth for me to become a ruler in Israel, and his goings forth are from of old, from days of yore *(long ago)*. — **Micah 5:2, LXX (NETS)**

*(The verse is numbered 5:2 in the NETS print and 5:1 in the older LXX verse-numbering; the words are the same.)*

---

### Observations

**(1)** **ἐκ σοῦ μοι ἐξελεύσεται** — *"Out of you, for Me, He shall come forth."* The Ruler **comes forth** from Bethlehem **for the Father** (**μοι** — *to/for Me*). The Son's appearing in Bethlehem is **for the Father's purpose**, given over to the Father's mission.

**(2)** **αἱ ἔξοδοι αὐτοῦ ἀπ᾽ ἀρχῆς ἐξ ἡμερῶν αἰῶνος** — *"His goings-forth are from the beginning, from days of old/eternity."*

The Greek phrasing is plural — **αἱ ἔξοδοι** — *the goings-forth.* This indicates **multiple comings-forth** of the Son:
- His **begetting** before the foundations of the earth (Prov 8:24-25; Ps 109:3 LXX).
- His subsequent **appearances** in Old Testament theophanies (which we will examine next).
- His ultimate **going forth from Bethlehem** in the incarnation.

The phrase **ἀπ᾽ ἀρχῆς ἐξ ἡμερῶν αἰῶνος** — *"from the beginning, from days of the age"* — places His origin far back, before days as men count them. This corresponds exactly to **πρὸ τοῦ αἰῶνος ἐθεμελίωσέν με** of Proverbs 8:23.

---

### Two witnesses for Micah 5:2 as a Messianic prophecy of the Son:

**Witness #1 — Matthew 2:3-6 (Geneva):**

> When King Herod heard this, he was troubled, and all Jerusalem with him. And gathering together all the chief Priests and Scribes of the people, he asked of them, where Christ should be born. And they said unto him, At Beth-leem in Judea: for so it is written by the Prophet, And thou Beth-leem in the land of Judah, art not the least among the Princes of Judah: For out of thee shall come the governor that shall feed that my people Israel. — **Matthew 2:3-6, 1599 Geneva**

The scribes of Israel — independently of Christian interpretation — already understood Micah 5:2 as referring to **the Christ.** Matthew, by inspiration, confirms it.

**Witness #2 — John 7:42 (Geneva):**

> Saith not the Scripture that that Christ shall come of the seed of David, and out of the town of Beth-leem, where David was? — **John 7:42, 1599 Geneva**

**Witness #2 established:** The common Jewish expectation, from Micah 5:2, was that the Christ would come out of Bethlehem.

---

### What Micah 5:2 adds to our portrait of the Son:

1. He is to be born in **Bethlehem** — a definite, prophesied earthly location.
2. He is a **Ruler** for Israel — given by the Father, **for** the Father (**μοι**).
3. His **goings-forth** (plural) extend back **from days of old / of the age** — He has a prior history before His birth in Bethlehem.

---

## **CONVERGENCE: What We Now Have Established**

Putting our four pillars together — **Psalm 2 + Psalm 109/110 + Proverbs 8 + Micah 5:2** — Scripture testifies that the Son:

| Truth | Witnesses |
|---|---|
| Is **begotten** of the Father | Ps 2:7; Ps 109:3 LXX; Prov 8:25 |
| Has an **origin** in the Father (no co-eternity in the Nicene sense) | Prov 8:22-25; Mic 5:2 (LXX) |
| Existed **before the world was made** | Prov 8:22-30; Mic 5:2 |
| Was the Father's **Wisdom** in fitting the creation together | Prov 8:27-30; 1 Cor 1:24; Col 1:16 |
| Has **multiple goings-forth** culminating in Bethlehem | Mic 5:2 |
| Is **made Lord and Christ** by the Father at His resurrection | Ps 2:7 + Acts 13:33; Ps 110:1 + Acts 2:36 |
| Is **forever Priest** by the Father's oath | Ps 110:4 + Heb 5:5-6 |
| Sits at the Father's **right hand**, distinct from Him | Ps 110:1; Matt 22:44 |

---

## **The Doctrinal Picture — Strictly From Scripture**

The Son of the One God:
- **Was brought forth** by the Father before time and the creation (**γεννᾷ με**, **ἔκτισέν με**, **ἐθεμελίωσέν με**).
- **Was with the Father** as His Wisdom at the work of creation (Prov 8; cf. John 1:1 — **πρὸς τὸν Θεόν**).
- **Came forth** in the fullness of time, born of the virgin in Bethlehem (Mic 5:2; Gen 3:15; Isa 7:14; Gal 4:4).
- **Was begotten in power** as Son of God by the resurrection (Ps 2:7; Rom 1:4; Acts 13:33).
- **Was seated** at the Father's right hand, made both Lord and Christ (Ps 110:1; Acts 2:36).
- **Is Priest forever** by oath of the Father (Ps 110:4; Heb 7:21).

**At every point, the Father is the Source, the Sender, the Appointer, the Anointer, the Begetter, the Glorifier.** The Son is the brought-forth, the sent, the appointed, the anointed, the begotten, the glorified — **and acts always for the Father's purposes** (Mic 5:2: **μοι** — *for Me*).

The Shema stands. Isaiah 44-46 stands. **The Father is the One God.** The Son is His Only-Begotten — distinct, beloved, exalted, but never a second God, never a co-equal, never the Father.

---

## **A Question Now Properly Opened**

Micah 5:2 spoke of the Son's **goings-forth** (plural) **from the beginning**. We have accounted for:
- His begetting (Prov 8 / Ps 109:3 LXX).
- His birth in Bethlehem (Mic 5:2 / Isa 7:14).

But what of the **intermediate goings-forth** — His appearances in the Old Testament between His begetting and His birth?

Scripture testifies to a Person called **the Angel of the LORD** (**ὁ ἄγγελος Κυρίου** — literally, *the Messenger of the LORD*) who appears repeatedly — to Hagar, Abraham, Jacob, Moses, Gideon, Manoah — and who *speaks as God*, is *worshipped*, yet is *sent from God*. Is this Person the pre-incarnate Son — one of the **ἔξοδοι** of Micah 5:2?
