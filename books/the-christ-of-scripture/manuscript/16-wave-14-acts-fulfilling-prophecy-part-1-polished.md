## **Jesus’ Acts During His Ministry — Part 1: Acts Fulfilling Prophecy**

> Keep those in mind… we will get to each one. But before we do, and in order to be thorough- let us also take note of Jesus’s acts during his ministry that tell us much of his person and fulfilled prophecy(s) both before and after his death, burial and resurrection.

This is the proper next step — for our study has so far moved largely through the *prophetic posits* of the Son (OT prophecies and their NT fulfillments at His death/burial/resurrection), but we have not yet gathered the *acts of His ministry* — the things He **did** and **said** during His life that:

1. **Reveal His Person** (His authority, character, relation to the Father, self-identifications).
2. **Fulfilled prophecy** during His ministry (not just at His death).
3. **Establish patterns** that the Apocalypse and later prophecies will draw upon.

Let me proceed under our refined modus operandi — examining the whole counsel, giving the LXX/Greek and 1599 Geneva, citing all relevant cross-witnesses, and being honest about what is strictly established versus inferred.

I will organize this by **categories of His ministerial acts**, since chronology would scatter the patterns. We will examine:

- **Part 1:** Acts that fulfilled specific prophecies (His birth, ministry, and pre-Passion fulfillments)
- **Part 2:** His self-identifications (the "I am" sayings and titles He took)
- **Part 3:** Acts that revealed His authority (forgiving sins, commanding nature, raising the dead, giving the Spirit)
- **Part 4:** His relation to the Father (His words about His mission, His will, His subordination)
- **Part 5:** Post-resurrection acts that revealed His person and commissioned the Apostles

This will be a substantial examination. Let us begin.

# **PART 1 — ACTS FULFILLING PROPHECY DURING HIS MINISTRY**

## **1.1 — Born in Bethlehem of a Virgin**

### **Isaiah 7:14, NETS:**

> Therefore the Lord himself will give you a sign. Look, the virgin shall be with child and bear a son, and you shall name him Emmanouel. — **Isaiah 7:14 LXX, NETS**
---

### **Micah 5:2 LXX (5:1), NETS (already examined):**

> And you, O Bethleem *(Bethlehem)*, house of Ephratha *(Ephrathah)*, are very few in number to be among the thousands of Ioudas *(Judah)*; one from you shall come forth for me to become a ruler in Israel, and his goings forth are from of old, from days of yore *(long ago)*. — **Micah 5:2 LXX (5:1), NETS**
---

### **Cross-witness — Matthew 1:22-23, 1599 Geneva:**

> And all this was done that it might be fulfilled which is spoken of the Lord by the Prophet, saying, Behold, a virgin shall be with child, and shall bear a son, and they shall call his name Emmanuel, which is by interpretation, God with us. — **Matthew 1:22-23, 1599 Geneva**
---

### **Matthew 2:5-6** — explicitly cites Micah 5:2 of Jesus’ birth in Bethlehem (already examined).

### Observation

**(1)** Matthew explicitly cites the LXX **ἡ παρθένος** (*the virgin*) — confirming the LXX reading against later attempts to render *"young woman."* Per our maxim #1, the God-breathed text Jesus’ Apostles used reads **παρθένος**.

**(2)** **Ἐμμανουήλ** is interpreted by Matthew as **Μεθ’ ἡμῶν ὁ Θεός** — *"With us, God."* The Son is "God with us" — meaning, the One in whom God is present with His people. This is a profound title that we should not pass over but also not overstate — it identifies Jesus as the embodied presence of God-with-His-people, not as a separate God.

## **1.2 — Called Out of Egypt**

### **Hosea 11:1, NETS:**

> For Israel was an infant, and I loved him, and out of Egypt I recalled his children. — **Hosea 11:1 LXX (11:1 MT), NETS**
---

### **Cross-witness — Matthew 2:14-15, 1599 Geneva:**

> So he arose and took the babe and his mother by night, and departed into Egypt, And was there unto the death of Herod, that that might be fulfilled, which is spoken of the Lord by the Prophet, saying, Out of Egypt have I called my son. — **Matthew 2:14-15, 1599 Geneva**
---

### Observation

Matthew identifies Jesus as **the Son** whom the Father called out of Egypt — fulfilling typologically what Israel-as-son had been (Israel = corporate son; Jesus = the True Son). This is **typology**, not simple prediction-fulfillment. The Apostle, by inspiration, sees Jesus recapitulating Israel’s history.

## **1.3 — The Voice in the Wilderness**

### **Isaiah 40:3, NETS:**

> A voice of one crying out in the wilderness: “Prepare the way of the Lord; make straight the paths of our God. — **Isaiah 40:3 LXX, NETS**
---

### **Cross-witness — Matthew 3:1-3, 1599 Geneva:**

> For this is he of whom it is spoken by the Prophet Esaias, saying, The voice of him that crieth in the wilderness, Prepare ye the way of the Lord: make his paths straight. — **Matthew 3:1-3, 1599 Geneva**
---

**Mark 1:2-3; Luke 3:4-6; John 1:23** — all identify John the Baptist as this voice, **preparing the way of the LORD**.

### Observation of significant weight

**Isaiah 40:3 says: "Prepare the way of the LORD (κυρίου)."** The Gospels apply this to John the Baptist preparing the way of **Jesus**. This is a strong identification: Jesus is the One *whose* way is prepared, and the One being prepared for is called **Κύριος** in Isaiah.

This does not automatically mean Jesus is identical with **YHWH** — but it does mean **the One coming in the LORD’s name and authority**, **bearing the LORD’s Name** (cf. Phil 2:9: the Name above every name given to the Son). The way of the LORD is prepared because the LORD is acting *through* His Christ.

## **1.4 — Galilee of the Nations**

### **Isaiah 9:1-2 LXX (8:23-9:1), NETS:**

> Do this first; do it quickly, O country of Zaboulon, the land of Nephthalime, and the rest who inhabit the seashore and beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the nations, the parts of Judea. O you people who walk in darkness, see a great light! O you who live in the country and in the shadow of death, light will shine on you! — **Isaiah 9:1-2 LXX (8:23-9:1), NETS**
---

### **Cross-witness — Matthew 4:14-16, 1599 Geneva:**

> That it might be fulfilled which was spoken by Isaiah the Prophet, saying, The land of Zebulun, and the land of Nephthali by the way of the sea, beyond Jordan, Galilee of the Gentiles: The people which sat in darkness, saw great light: and to them which sat in the region, and shadow of death, light is risen up. — **Matthew 4:14-16, 1599 Geneva**
---

### Observation

Jesus’ choice to base His ministry in Capernaum (Galilee of the Gentiles) is explicitly identified by Matthew as fulfilling Isaiah 9:1-2 — **the Light dawning** on those in darkness. This is the same "Light" theme of John 1:4-9 — confirming that the Light/Word of John 1 is Jesus in His historical ministry.

## **1.5 — Healing, Casting Out Demons, Bearing Our Infirmities**

### **Isaiah 53:4 LXX (already examined, seated at the Suffering-Servant section):**

> This one bears our sins and suffers pain for us, and we accounted him to be in trouble and calamity and ill-treatment. — **Isaiah 53:4 LXX, NETS**
---

### **Cross-witness — Matthew 8:16-17 (already cited):** healing as fulfillment of Isa 53:4.

### **Isaiah 35:5-6, NETS:**

> Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall hear; then the lame shall leap like a deer, and the tongue of stammerers shall be clear, because water has broken forth in the wilderness and a gully in a thirsty land; — **Isaiah 35:5-6, NETS**
---

### **Cross-witness — Matthew 11:2-5, 1599 Geneva:**

> And when John heard in the prison the works of Christ, he sent two of his disciples, and said unto him, Art thou he that should come, or shall we look for another? And Jesus answering, said unto them, Go, and show John, what things ye hear and see. The blind receive sight, and the halt do walk: the lepers are cleansed, and the deaf hear, the dead are raised up, and the poor receive the Gospel. — **Matthew 11:2-5, 1599 Geneva**
---

### Observation of decisive weight

When John the Baptist asks **"Are You the Coming One?"** — Jesus does **not** say "Yes, I am." Instead, He answers by **listing the works of Isaiah 35:5-6** and **Isaiah 61:1**. Jesus’ self-attestation is **by His prophesied works.** He lets His acts fulfilling prophecy answer the question of His identity.

This is highly instructive for our study: **Jesus’ acts during His ministry were themselves a prophetic testimony.** His miracles were not random demonstrations of power — they were specifically the prophesied works of the Coming One.

## **1.6 — Anointed to Preach Good News (Isaiah 61)**

### **Isaiah 61:1-2, NETS:**

> The spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he has anointed me; he has sent me to bring good news to the poor, to heal the brokenhearted, to proclaim release to the captives and recovery of sight to the blind, to summon the acceptable year of the Lord and the day of retribution, to comfort all who mourn — **Isaiah 61:1-2, NETS**
---

### **Cross-witness — Luke 4:16-21, 1599 Geneva:**

> And he came to Nazareth where he had been brought up... he stood up to read. And there was delivered unto him the book of the Prophet Isaiah... He found the place where it was written, The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me, that I should preach the Gospel to the poor: he hath sent me, that I should heal the broken hearted, that I should preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, that I should set at liberty them that are bruised, And that I should preach the acceptable year of the Lord... Then he began to say unto them, This day is the Scripture fulfilled in your ears. — **Luke 4:16-21, 1599 Geneva**
---

### Observation of supreme importance

**Jesus’ first recorded sermon in His own town** consists of:
1. Reading Isaiah 61:1-2 LXX.
2. Declaring: **"This day is this Scripture fulfilled in your ears."**

This is **Jesus’ own direct self-identification** as the Spirit-anointed Servant. He stops reading at *"the acceptable year of the LORD"* — *not* continuing to *"the day of vengeance of our God,"* because that day is yet future. His first coming is the year of grace; the day of vengeance belongs to His return.

This is *Jesus’ own claim* that the prophet’s *"Spirit of the LORD is upon Me"* is *His own voice*. He is the Anointed.

## **1.7 — Triumphal Entry**

### **Zechariah 9:9, NETS:**

> Rejoice greatly, O daughter Sion! Proclaim, O daughter Ierousalem! Behold, your king comes to you, just and salvific is he, meek and riding on a beast of burden and a young foal. — **Zechariah 9:9 LXX, NETS**
---

### **Cross-witness — Matthew 21:4-5, 1599 Geneva:**

> All this was done that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the Prophet, saying, Tell ye the daughter of Sion, Behold, thy King cometh unto thee, meek, and sitting upon an ass, and a colt, the foal of an ass used to the yoke. — **Matthew 21:4-5, 1599 Geneva**
---

### **John 12:14-15** — same fulfillment cited.

### Observation

**Jesus deliberately arranged this fulfillment** — He sent disciples to acquire the colt for the express purpose of riding it into Jerusalem (Matt 21:1-3). This is not incidental; it is **Jesus’ own act of self-prophetic-attestation** — He publicly enters Jerusalem in the manner of the prophesied King.

## **1.9 — Pierced — They Shall Look On Him Whom They Pierced**

### **Zechariah 12:10, NETS:**

> And I will pour out a spirit of grace and compassion on the house of Dauid and on the inhabitants of Ierousalem, and they shall look to me because they have danced triumphantly, and they shall mourn for him with a mourning as for a loved one, and they shall be pained with pain as for a firstborn. — **Zechariah 12:10 LXX, NETS**
---

### **Cross-witness — John 19:34-37, 1599 Geneva:**

> But one of the soldiers with a spear pierced his side, and forthwith came there out blood and water... For these things were done, that the Scripture should be fulfilled... And again another Scripture saith, They shall see him whom they have thrust through. — **John 19:34-37, 1599 Geneva**
---

### **Revelation 1:7, 1599 Geneva:**

> Behold, he cometh with clouds, and every eye shall see him: yea, even they which pierced him through: and all kindreds of the earth shall wail before him, Even so, Amen. — **Revelation 1:7, 1599 Geneva**
---

### Observation of great weight

**Two NT witnesses** (John 19 and Rev 1) bind **Zechariah 12:10** directly to Jesus’ piercing on the cross and to His future return. This is one of the most important prophetic texts in our study because **it appears at both ends — fulfilled at the crucifixion AND to be fulfilled at His return.**

**A critical note on the Greek:** The LXX of Zech 12:10 reads **ἐπιβλέψονται πρός με** — *"they shall look upon Me"* — and **κόψονται ἐπ’ αὐτόν** — *"they shall mourn for Him."* The shift from *"Me"* to *"Him"* within the same verse is striking. The LORD speaks (*"they shall look upon Me"*) — yet they mourn for *Him* (a third person). This is the kind of Father-Son interplay we have been noting throughout: the LORD’s piercing is the piercing of His Sent One, and the mourning is for that Sent One.
