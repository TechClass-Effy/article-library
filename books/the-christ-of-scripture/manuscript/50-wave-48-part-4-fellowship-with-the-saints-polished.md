# **PART 4 — FELLOWSHIP WITH THE SAINTS**

The believer is not designed for isolation. The Father has placed him in the Body of Christ, where he both ministers to others and is ministered to.

## **The Foundation: We Belong to One Another**

### **Romans 12:5 — Geneva 1599:**

*"So we being many are one body in Christ, and every one, one anothers members."*

The believer is **a member of the Body** — and **members of one another.** The body image insists on mutual interdependence.

---

## **The Necessity of Assembling**

### **Hebrews 10:24-25 — Geneva 1599:**

*"And let us consider one another, to provoke unto love, and to good works, Not forsaking the fellowship that we have among ourselves, as the manner of some is: but let us exhort one another, and that so much the more, because ye see that the day draweth near."*

---

### Observations

**(1)** The believers are to **consider one another** — paying attention to one another's needs.

**(2)** The purpose is **to stir up love and good works** — to provoke one another (παροξυσμός — a sharp stirring up) toward what is good.

**(3) Negative command:** *"Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together."* Some were already abandoning the gathering. The author warns against this.

**(4) Positive command:** *"Exhorting one another."* The believers are to encourage and admonish one another.

**(5) Urgency:** *"As ye see the day approaching."* The Lord's return makes faithful gathering more urgent, not less.

---

## **The "One Another" Commands**

The NT contains over fifty "one another" commands — instructions for how believers are to relate to one another in the Body. Major examples:

- **Love one another** (John 13:34; Rom 12:10; 13:8; 1 Thess 4:9; 1 Pet 1:22; 1 John 3:11, 23; 4:7, 11-12).
- **Forgive one another** (Eph 4:32; Col 3:13).
- **Bear with one another** (Eph 4:2; Col 3:13).
- **Bear one another's burdens** (Gal 6:2).
- **Comfort one another** (1 Thess 4:18; 5:11).
- **Encourage / exhort one another** (1 Thess 5:11; Heb 3:13; 10:25).
- **Build up one another** (Rom 14:19; 1 Thess 5:11).
- **Serve one another** (Gal 5:13; 1 Pet 4:10).
- **Submit to one another** (Eph 5:21; 1 Pet 5:5).
- **Honor one another** (Rom 12:10).
- **Be hospitable to one another** (1 Pet 4:9).
- **Pray for one another** (James 5:16).
- **Confess sins to one another** (James 5:16).
- **Teach and admonish one another** (Col 3:16).
- **Greet one another** (Rom 16:16; 1 Cor 16:20; 2 Cor 13:12; 1 Pet 5:14).
- **Do not bite and devour one another** (Gal 5:15).
- **Do not lie to one another** (Col 3:9).
- **Do not speak evil of one another** (James 4:11).

**This is the texture of biblical fellowship.** Christianity is not a solitary pursuit; it is a body life.

---

## **Practical Implications**

**1. Be committed to a local assembly.** The believer needs the regular gathering of the saints — for teaching, worship, fellowship, the Lord's Supper, prayer, and mutual encouragement.

**2. Develop deep relationships with a few fellow believers** — those with whom you can pray, confess sin, bear burdens, exhort.

**3. Use your gifts to serve the Body** — every believer has gifts (Rom 12; 1 Cor 12; Eph 4; 1 Pet 4:10-11). Discover yours and use them.

**4. Be a source of encouragement.** In a world of discouragement, the believer who consistently encourages is a deep gift to the Body.

**5. Bear with weaknesses.** Every believer has weaknesses, immaturities, and failures. Bear with one another in patience.

**6. Resist factionalism, gossip, and divisive speech.** These are the work of the flesh and destroy the Body's unity.

**7. Submit to godly leaders** (Heb 13:17). The Father has appointed shepherds for the flock; submit to their oversight as those who watch for your souls.
