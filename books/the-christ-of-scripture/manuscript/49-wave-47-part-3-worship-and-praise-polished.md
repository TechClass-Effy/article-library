# **PART 3 — WORSHIP AND PRAISE**

Worship is the believer's response of adoration to the Father, through the Son, in the Spirit. It is both an inward attitude and outward expression.

## **The Foundation: Worship in Spirit and Truth**

### **John 4:23-24 — Geneva 1599:**

*"But the hour cometh, and now is, when the true worshippers shall worship the Father in spirit, and truth: for the Father requireth even such to worship him. God is a Spirit, and they that worship him, must worship him in spirit and truth."*

---

### Observations

We already examined this passage extensively under Part E (the Father as Eternal Spirit). Let us now note its implications for worship:

**(1) Worship is to the Father.** Jesus identifies the Father as the recipient of true worship. This honors our foundation.

**(2) Worship is in Spirit and truth.**
- **In Spirit** — in the believer's regenerated spirit, indwelt by the Holy Spirit, not merely outward observance.
- **In truth** — in conformity with the truth God has revealed, not in falsehood or self-invented worship.

**(3) The Father seeks such worshippers.** The Father is not indifferent. He **seeks** those who worship Him truly.

---

## **The Sacrifice of Praise**

### **Hebrews 13:15 — Geneva 1599:**

*Let us therefore by him offer the sacrifice of praise always to God, that is, the fruit of the lips, which confess his Name.*

---

### Observation

Praise is **a sacrifice** — offered **through Him** (the Son) **to God** (the Father). The "fruit of the lips" confessing His Name is acceptable to the Father through the Son.

This is the new covenant fulfillment of the OT sacrificial system: not animals on an altar, but praise from the heart of the redeemed, offered through the Mediator.

---

## **The Modes of Worship**

### **Ephesians 5:18-20 — Geneva 1599:**

*...but be fulfilled *(filled)* with the Spirit, Speaking unto yourselves in psalms, and hymns, and spiritual songs, singing, and making melody to the Lord in your hearts, Giving thanks always for all things unto God even the Father, in the Name of our Lord Jesus Christ,*

---

### Observations

**(1) Worship is a fruit of being filled with the Spirit.** The Spirit-filled believer naturally speaks in spiritual songs, sings, gives thanks.

**(2) Three categories of song:**
- **ψαλμοί** — psalms (the OT Psalter).
- **ὕμνοι** — hymns (compositions of praise).
- **ᾠδαὶ πνευματικαί** — spiritual songs (Spirit-prompted songs).

**(3) Singing is to the Lord — in the heart.** External singing is the overflow of internal melody. **The heart sings first; the lips follow.**

**(4) Thanksgiving in all things, in His Name, to the Father.** The pattern again: **through the Son, to the Father.**

---

## **Worship in Daily Life**

Worship is not confined to formal services. The whole of life is worship, as Romans 12:1 established. But there are particular acts of worship:

- **Daily morning thanksgiving** — beginning the day with adoration.
- **Singing throughout the day** — psalms, hymns, spiritual songs as the Spirit prompts.
- **Acknowledging God in all decisions** — *"In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths"* (Prov 3:6 Geneva).
- **Gathering with the saints** — corporate worship in the assembly.
- **The Lord's Supper** — the great act of remembering and communing.
- **Acts of service** — service rendered to others as worship to the Father.
