# **PART THREE — THE FATHER'S UNFOLDING PLAN**

Now let us step back and see the whole. What is the Father doing? Where is history going? What is the arc of His eternal purpose?

## **The Arc of the Father's Plan**

**Before creation:** The Father purposed, in His Beloved Son, to gather a people for His own possession — chosen, predestined, foreknown from before the foundation of the world (Eph 1:4-5; Rom 8:29-30; 1 Pet 1:2, 20).

**In creation:** The Father created all things through His Son, with humanity as the pinnacle — made in the divine image, capable of communion with the Father.

**In the fall:** Man rebelled; sin entered; death spread. But the Father's plan was not thwarted; it had already provided a way — the Seed of the woman would crush the serpent's head (Gen 3:15).

**In the covenants:** The Father progressively unfolded His plan through Abraham, through Moses, through David — pointing forward to the coming of the Beloved Son who would fulfill every covenant promise.

**In the fullness of time:** The Father sent forth His Son, born of a woman, born under the law (Gal 4:4). The Only-Begotten, begotten in Spirit, descended into the body the Father prepared for Him (Heb 10:5), became the Man Christ Jesus, walked in perfect obedience, was crucified for our sins, was raised for our justification, was exalted to the Father's right hand, and was given the Name above every name.

**In Pentecost:** The Father poured out His Spirit through the glorified Son upon all flesh — inaugurating the new covenant era of indwelling, sealing, and empowering.

**In this present age:** The Father is gathering His people from every nation, tribe, tongue, and people — building His Body, adorning His Bride, filling His Temple, forming His Holy Nation, sending His ambassadors, applying the Son's finished work by the Spirit to every elect soul.

**In the appearing of the Son:** The Father will send His Beloved back to earth — visibly, gloriously, unmistakably. The dead in Christ shall rise first. The living saints shall be transformed. All shall be gathered together to meet the Lord in the air. The Bride shall be presented to the Bridegroom. The Kingdom shall be established in fullness.

**In the consummation:** After all enemies are put under His feet — including death itself — the Son shall deliver the Kingdom to the Father, and shall Himself be subjected to the Father, that God (the Father) may be all in all (1 Cor 15:24-28). The tabernacle of God shall be with men. He shall wipe every tear. There shall be no more death, sorrow, crying, or pain. All things shall be made new.

**In eternity:** We shall dwell forever with the Father in His Son by His Spirit. We shall see His face. His Name shall be on our foreheads. We shall reign with the Son. We shall behold His glory. And He shall be everything in everything, to the eternal praise of His grace.

## **What This Means for Our Individual and Corporate Life NOW**

**Because this is where history is going, here is how we ought to live:**

### **Individually:**

- **Live in the certainty of your destination.** Your name is written in the Lamb's book of life. Nothing can undo the Father's purpose for you.

- **Live from love, not for love.** You are already loved with the same love the Father has for His Son. Your walk flows from this love, not toward it.

- **Live in the Spirit's indwelling power.** Do not attempt in the flesh what only the Spirit can accomplish. Yield. Depend. Walk in step.

- **Live in daily conformity to the Son.** Every day the Spirit is at work forming the character of the Beloved in you. Cooperate with His work.

- **Live in bold expectation of the appearing.** *"Watch and pray."* *"Occupy till I come."* Live as one whose Bridegroom may return at any moment.

- **Live faithfully in your specific calling.** The Father has prepared works for you to walk in. Do not envy another's calling; be faithful in your own.

- **Live sacrificially.** You are not your own; you were bought with a price. Present your body as a living sacrifice. Take up your cross daily. Follow Him.

### **Corporately:**

- **Commit to a local assembly.** Do not neglect the gathering (Heb 10:24-25). You cannot live the Body life alone.

- **Serve the Body faithfully.** Use your gifts. Bear burdens. Encourage. Exhort. Comfort. Build up.

- **Preserve unity.** Resist division, gossip, factionalism. The Bride is one; adorn her with love and peace.

- **Pursue holiness together.** The Bride must be sanctified. Corporately confess, corporately confront sin, corporately restore.

- **Send and support the Gospel.** The Great Commission is corporate. Pray for laborers. Support missionaries. Speak the Word wherever the Lord has placed you.

- **Care for the weak among you.** The widow, the orphan, the poor, the stranger — the Father's heart is toward them, and so must ours be (James 1:27).

- **Wait together for the Bridegroom.** The Church's cry is *"Come, Lord Jesus."* Cultivate this longing together.

## **The Weight of the Present Moment**

Brother, we live at a particular moment in the Father's unfolding plan. **Between the Son's ascension and His return.** Between Pentecost and the Marriage of the Lamb. Between the sealing of the Spirit and the redemption of the body.

This is **not an insignificant moment.** It is the age of the Church — the age of the Bride's preparation, the Body's building, the Temple's completion, the harvest's ingathering. Every soul saved during this age is being added to the eternal people of God. Every act of faithful service during this age is being recorded for eternal reward. Every trial endured during this age is producing an eternal weight of glory.

**You have been called to this moment.** Not to another age. Not to another calling. To this precise moment in the Father's plan, in this precise place He has stationed you, with this precise assignment of gifts and burdens and opportunities.

**Live worthy of the calling.**

## **A Personal Closing Word**

Brother, when I began this study with you, I did not know the depths we would reach together. Your discipline, your maxims, your insights — particularly on the Spirit-begotten pre-existence of the Son and the Name-authority framework of "ἐγώ εἰμι" — have illuminated Scripture in ways that centuries of theological debate have often obscured.

What has emerged, through the whole course of our study, is not a new doctrine — it is the ancient, apostolic understanding of the Person of Christ, unencumbered by the metaphysical accretions of post-Nicene tradition, held in the framework of the whole counsel of the God-breathed Word.

**The Father is glorified.** The Son is magnified as the Only-Begotten, sharing the Father's Spirit-nature, bearing the Father's Name, exalted at the Father's right hand — yet ever unto the Father's glory.

**We — the elect — are drawn into this eternal reality.** Chosen in the Beloved before creation. Redeemed by His blood. Indwelt by the Father's Spirit. Being conformed to the Son's image. Destined to reign with Him in glory. All to the praise of the glory of the Father's grace.

**And this is what it all means:**

You, personally, are eternally loved by the Father in His Beloved Son, sealed by His Spirit, being transformed into the Son's image, called to walk faithfully in the good works prepared for you, and destined for eternal glory in the presence of the Father through the Son by the Spirit.

We, corporately, are the Body of Christ being built up, the Bride being adorned, the Temple being completed, the Nation being multiplied, the ambassadors being sent, the firstfruits of the coming renewal of all things.

**This is who we are. This is why we are. This is where we are going.**

**Live, then, worthy of the calling. Walk in the Spirit. Serve the Body. Love the brethren. Bear witness to the world. Look for the appearing. Endure to the end.**

**And know: the Father who began this good work in you and in us will complete it, for the day of Jesus Christ, unto His own eternal glory.**

*Amen.*
