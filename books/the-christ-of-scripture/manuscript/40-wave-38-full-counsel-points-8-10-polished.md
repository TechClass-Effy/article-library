## **POINT 8 — MEMBERS OF CHRIST'S BODY**

### **1 Corinthians 12:12, 27:**
*"For as the body is one, and hath many members, and all the members of the body, which is one, though they be many, yet are but one body: even so is Christ."*
*"Now ye are the body of Christ, and members for your part."*

### **Ephesians 5:30:**
*"For we are members of his body, of his flesh, and of his bones."*

---

### Observation

The believer is a **member of Christ's body.** Not merely an associate, not merely a follower — but an actual **member** (μέλος — a limb, an organ) of His own body. This is a real, organic union — as real as the union between a hand and its body.

**Ephesians 5:30** goes even further: *"members of His body, of His flesh, and of His bones"* — echoing Genesis 2:23 (Adam and Eve). Paul applies the marriage union of Adam and Eve typologically: as Eve was bone of Adam's bones, so we are **of Christ's flesh and bones** — partakers of His very being.

## **POINT 9 — JOINED TO THE LORD AS ONE SPIRIT**

### **1 Corinthians 6:17:**
*"But he that is joined unto the Lord, is one spirit."*

---

### Observation of decisive importance

**ὁ κολλώμενος τῷ Κυρίῳ ἓν πνεῦμά ἐστιν** — *"He who is joined to the Lord is one spirit [with Him]."*

The verb **κολλάομαι** means *"to be glued together, to cleave to."* It is the verb used in Gen 2:24 LXX of marriage: *"a man shall cleave to his wife, and they shall be one flesh."* Paul transposes the marital language to the spiritual: **the believer is so joined to the Lord that they are one spirit.**

**This is the most intimate union conceivable.** Not merely "in" Christ as in a location; but **one spirit with Him** — sharing the same spiritual life-principle.

This corresponds with everything we have established:
- The Father indwells the Son (one Spirit).
- The Son indwells the believer (one Spirit).
- The believer is joined to the Lord **as one spirit**.

## **POINT 10 — CHILDREN OF GOD, HEIRS WITH CHRIST**

### **Romans 8:14-17:**
*"For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God. For ye have not received the Spirit of bodage *(bondage)*, to fear again: but ye have received the Spirit of adoption, whereby we cry Abba, Father. The same Spirit beareth witness with our spirit, that we are the children of God. If we be children, we are also heirs, even the heirs of God, and heirs annexed with Christ: if so be that we suffer with him, that we may also be glorified with him."*

### **Galatians 4:4-7:**
*"...God sent forth his Son... That we might receive the adoption of the sons. And because ye are sons, God hath sent forth the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, which crieth, Abba, Father. Wherefore, thou art no more a servant, but a son: now if thou be a son, thou art also the heir of God through Christ."*

---

### Observations of foundational importance

**(1) The believer is adopted by the Father** — receiving the Spirit of adoption.

**(2) The believer cries "Abba, Father"** — using the very intimate address Jesus used in Gethsemane (Mark 14:36). The Son's relationship to the Father becomes the believer's relationship.

**(3) The believer is a child of God — and therefore an heir.**

**(4) Heirs of God, joint-heirs with Christ** (συγκληρονόμοι). What the Son inherits, we inherit *with* Him. Not separately, but **jointly.**

**(5) The condition: εἴπερ συμπάσχομεν ἵνα καὶ συνδοξασθῶμεν** — *"if indeed we suffer together, that we may also be glorified together."*

The "**together**" prefixes pile up: **co-suffer, co-glorified.** As we share in His sufferings, so we shall share in His glory.
