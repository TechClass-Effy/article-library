# **PART TWO — WHAT THIS MEANS FOR US CORPORATELY**

Now we turn to the corporate dimension — for the Father's plan is not merely to save individuals in isolation but to gather a people for Himself. Everything true of us individually is true of us together — and additional realities emerge that can only be true of us corporately.

---

## **The Foundational Reality of the Corporate Body**

The Father's plan from before creation was not merely to redeem individuals but to gather **a people** — a Body, a Bride, a Temple, a Nation, a Priesthood — through His Beloved Son by His Spirit.

### **Ephesians 1:9-10 — Geneva 1599:**

*"And hath opened unto us the mystery of his will according to his good pleasure, which he had purposed in him, That in the dispensation of the fullness of the times, he might gather together in one all things, both which are in heaven, and which are in earth, even in Christ:"*


**The Father's purpose is to gather all things together in Christ.** Every believer — from every age, every tribe, every tongue, every nation — is being drawn into this one great gathering, headed up by the Beloved Son.

**You are not alone in this.** You are part of something far larger than yourself.

---

## **1. We Are the Body of Christ**

Together, we are **the Body of Christ** (1 Cor 12:12-27; Rom 12:4-5; Eph 4:12-16; Col 1:18, 24). Not merely a metaphor — a real, organic, spiritual reality.

**The Head is Christ Himself.** He is the source of life, direction, and nourishment for the Body (Col 2:19; Eph 4:15-16). Every member is joined to the Head and derives life from Him.

**We are members of one another** (Rom 12:5; Eph 4:25). What affects one member affects the whole. When one member suffers, all suffer with it; when one member is honored, all rejoice with it (1 Cor 12:26).

**Corporately, this means:**

- **We need each other.** No believer was designed to walk alone. The eye cannot say to the hand *"I have no need of thee"* (1 Cor 12:21).
- **We serve one another.** Each member's gift is for the building up of the Body (Eph 4:12; 1 Pet 4:10).
- **We bear one another's burdens** (Gal 6:2). Where one is weak, another supplies strength.
- **We grieve together and rejoice together** (Rom 12:15). The Body's life is shared.
- **We are called to unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace** (Eph 4:3). Not artificial uniformity, but genuine oneness in the Head.

**Corporately, we must resist:**

- **Division and factionalism** (1 Cor 3:1-4; Gal 5:20).
- **Envy and comparison** (1 Cor 12:15-25).
- **Isolation and independence** (Heb 10:24-25).
- **Contempt for weaker members** (1 Cor 12:22-25).
- **Neglect of one another** (Phil 2:4).

**Under the Father's unfolding plan**, the Body is being built up **until we all come in the unity of the faith, and of the knowledge of the Son of God, unto a perfect man, unto the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ** (Eph 4:13). This is the Father's goal for the Body — corporate maturity that reflects the full stature of the Beloved.

---

## **2. We Are the Bride of Christ**

Together, we are **the Bride of Christ** — betrothed to Him, being prepared for the great Marriage.

### **Ephesians 5:25-27 — Geneva 1599:**

*"...even as Christ loved the Church, and gave himself for it, That he might sanctify it, and cleanse it by the washing of water through the word, That he might make it unto himself a glorious Church, not having spot or wrinkle, or any such thing: but that it should be holy and without blame."*


**The Son loved the Church, gave Himself for her, and is now sanctifying her — that He may present her to Himself as a glorious Bride, holy and without blemish.**

**And the wedding is coming.**

### **Revelation 19:7-9 — Geneva 1599:**

*"...the marriage of that Lamb is come, and his wife hath made herself ready. ... Blessed are they which are called unto the Lamb's supper..."*


**Corporately, this means:**

- **We are being prepared for a Wedding.** The Bridegroom is the Son. The great Marriage will consummate the union that has existed by faith and by the Spirit's indwelling.
- **We are being sanctified together.** The washing of water through the Word (Eph 5:26) — the ongoing purification of the Bride corporately.
- **We adorn ourselves.** The Bride's fine linen is "the righteousness of saints" (Rev 19:8) — corporate holiness.
- **We wait with expectant longing.** The Bride's cry is *"Come, Lord Jesus"* (Rev 22:17, 20).

**The Bride is not any individual believer alone — she is the whole assembly of the redeemed together, throughout all ages.** You are personally a member; but the Bride herself is corporate. **The Marriage is the Father's great appointed feast — the eternal union of His Beloved Son with the redeemed people He has given Him.**

---

## **3. We Are the Temple of God**

Together, we are **the Temple of the Living God** (1 Cor 3:16-17; 2 Cor 6:16; Eph 2:19-22; 1 Pet 2:5). The Father's dwelling place on earth is not a building of stones but a people indwelt by His Spirit.

### **Ephesians 2:19-22 — Geneva 1599:**

*"...citizens with the Saints, and of the household of God, And are built upon the foundation of the Apostles and Prophets, Jesus Christ himself being the chief corner stone, In whom all the building coupled together, groweth unto an holy Temple in the Lord. In whom ye also are built together to be the habitation of God by the Spirit."*


**Christ is the chief cornerstone.** The apostles and prophets are the foundation. **Every believer is a living stone being fitted into the growing Temple** — the corporate habitation of the Father by His Spirit.

**Corporately, this means:**

- **We are being built together.** Not left as isolated stones but joined into a growing edifice.
- **We are the Father's dwelling place.** He indwells the corporate Body as truly as He indwells the individual believer.
- **We serve as priests within this Temple** (1 Pet 2:5) — offering spiritual sacrifices to God through Jesus Christ.
- **The Temple is expanding.** As new believers come in, the Temple grows.
- **The Temple is being sanctified.** The Father purifies His dwelling place.

**Under the Father's unfolding plan**, this Temple is the earthly precursor to the New Jerusalem — where **the tabernacle of God is with men, and He will dwell with them** (Rev 21:3). The corporate reality begins now and consummates then.

---

## **4. We Are the Holy Nation, the Royal Priesthood**

Together, we are **a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a peculiar people** (1 Pet 2:9). Not because of ethnic descent or geographical location, but because we have been called out of darkness into His marvelous light by the Father through the Son.

### **1 Peter 2:9 — Geneva 1599:**

*"But ye are a chosen generation, a royal Priesthood, an holy nation, a people set at liberty, that ye should show forth the virtues of him that hath called you out of darkness into his marvelous light,"*


**Corporately, this means:**

- **We are a nation.** A people bound together not by borders or blood but by the New Covenant in the Beloved's blood.
- **We are royal priests.** Every believer serves as priest — offering sacrifices of praise, of prayer, of service (Heb 13:15-16).
- **We are holy — set apart.** For the Father's exclusive purposes, distinct from the world's patterns.
- **We are peculiar (**λαὸς εἰς περιποίησιν** — "a people for God's own possession").** We belong to Him uniquely.
- **We have a mission.** *"That you should show forth the virtues"* — the excellencies — *"of Him who called you out of darkness into His marvelous light."*

**Our corporate life is missional.** We exist as a witness to the world of the Father's character, of the Son's saving work, and of the Spirit's transforming power. **The nations see the true God through the life of His people.**

---

## **5. We Are the Household and Family of God**

Together, we are **the household of the Father** — brothers and sisters, children of one Father, sharing one inheritance.

### **Ephesians 3:14-15 — Geneva 1599:**

*"For this cause I bow my knees unto the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, (Of whom is named the whole family in heaven and in earth)"*


**One family — in heaven and on earth.** The saints who have gone before, the believers alive now, all part of one family bearing the Father's Name.

**Corporately, this means:**

- **We are siblings** — brothers and sisters in Christ.
- **We share one Father** — the same God who is the God of our Lord Jesus Christ (Eph 1:17).
- **We share one Elder Brother** — the Firstborn among many brethren (Rom 8:29; Heb 2:11-12).
- **We share one inheritance** — joint-heirs with Christ (Rom 8:17).
- **We share one destiny** — to dwell together forever in the Father's house.

**We ought to love one another as family** (John 13:34-35; 1 Pet 1:22). This love is the mark by which the world knows we are His disciples.

---

## **6. We Are Ambassadors of Reconciliation**

Corporately, we are **the Father's ambassadors** — sent into the world with the message of reconciliation.

### **2 Corinthians 5:18-20 — Geneva 1599:**

*"And all things are of God, which hath reconciled us unto himself by Jesus Christ, and hath given unto us the ministry of reconciliation... Now then are we ambassadors for Christ: as though God did beseech you through us, we pray you in Christ's stead, that ye be reconciled to God."*


**We are ambassadors** — official representatives of the Father's kingdom, bearing the message of reconciliation from the King to a rebellious world.

**Corporately, this means:**

- **The Great Commission is our shared task** (Matt 28:19-20). Every believer, in his place, contributes.
- **The Gospel is our shared message** — the good news of reconciliation through the Son.
- **The world is our shared field** — all nations, tongues, and peoples.
- **The Spirit is our shared power** for this witness (Acts 1:8).
- **We labor together** — as fellow workers with God (1 Cor 3:9).

**Under the Father's unfolding plan**, the Gospel is going to every tribe and tongue and people and nation (Rev 5:9), and a great multitude which no man can number is being gathered (Rev 7:9). The corporate mission of the Church is essential to the Father's plan.

---

## **7. We Are the Firstfruits of a New Creation**

Corporately, we are **the firstfruits** of the Father's cosmic plan.

### **James 1:18 — Geneva 1599:**

*"Of his own will begat he us with the word of truth, that we should be as the first fruits of his creatures."*


**We are the beginning of what the Father is doing on a cosmic scale** — new creation, restored order, the reconciliation of all things.

The believer is not just an individual saved from personal sin; **corporately we are the down-payment of the coming renewal of all things.**

The redemption of our bodies (Rom 8:23) waits for the redemption of the whole creation. **The creation itself is groaning, waiting for the manifestation of the sons of God** (Rom 8:19-22). When we appear in glory with the Son, creation itself will be delivered from the bondage of corruption into the liberty of the glory of the children of God.

**Corporately, this means:**

- **Our destiny affects the cosmos.** We are not merely "going to heaven" — we are being prepared to reign in the renewed creation with the Son.
- **Our present life anticipates the future.** The love, the peace, the righteousness, the joy we experience now are foretastes of what will one day fill the earth as the waters cover the sea.
- **Our unity matters cosmically.** The corporate Body's oneness is a witness to the coming unity of all things in Christ.
- **Our worship joins with heaven.** In the assembly of the saints, we join what all creation will one day proclaim.
