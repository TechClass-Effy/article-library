# **THE CULMINATING BEAUTY OF OUR STUDY**

Brother, having now walked through the whole study together — from the earliest prophecies through the consummation, from the Person of Christ through our identification with Him, from the practical disciplines through the difficult passages — I want to reflect on what we have discovered.

**Our maxims have carried us to a place where the Person of Christ shines forth with unclouded clarity:**

**The Father is the One True God** — eternal Spirit, invisible, immortal, unapproachable in His essential glory (John 4:24; John 17:3; 1 Tim 6:16). He is the Source of all things, the Beginning and the End, the Alpha and Omega in His absolute sense.

**The Son is the Only-Begotten of the Father** — begotten in Spirit before creation, sharing the Father's Spirit-nature, bearing the Father's Name given to Him, existing in living, personal communion with the Father in the Spirit-realm. He is the Father's Image, His Word, His Wisdom, His creative Agent. Through Him the Father made all things.

**When the fullness of time came**, the Father prepared a body for the Son (Heb 10:5), and He descended from heaven, born of the virgin, becoming the Man Christ Jesus. He walked in the Father's authority, spoke the Father's words, did the Father's works, and speaking with the Father's Name-authority He could invoke "ἐγώ εἰμι" — the Father's own self-identifying formula — because He was the One in whom the Father's Name dwelt.

**He was obedient unto death — even the death of the cross** — the ultimate humbling of the Only-Begotten Name-Bearer. He was raised, glorified, and exalted by the Father to His right hand. The Father gave Him the Name above every name (Phil 2:9), so that at the Name of Jesus every knee should bow — to the glory of God the Father.

**He now reigns as the exalted Man Christ Jesus** — bearing the fullness of the Godhead bodily forever (Col 2:9), the one Mediator between God and men, our Great High Priest, always living to make intercession for us. He is the King of kings and Lord of lords — but ever unto the Father's glory.

**And in the consummation, He will deliver the Kingdom to God the Father** — that God (the Father) may be all in all (1 Cor 15:24-28). The eternal Father-Son order is preserved forever, with the Son eternally the Beloved Only-Begotten, and the Father eternally the One God supreme.

---

**Meanwhile, we — the elect — are drawn into this eternal reality:**

- Chosen in Him before the foundation of the world (Eph 1:4).
- Given to the Son by the Father (John 17:6, 24).
- Redeemed by His precious blood (1 Pet 1:18-19).
- Baptized into His death, buried with Him, raised with Him, seated with Him at the Father's right hand (Rom 6:3-5; Eph 2:5-6).
- Indwelt by the Father's Spirit — sealed forever unto the day of redemption (Eph 1:13-14; 4:30).
- Bearing the Father's Name (Rev 22:4).
- Being conformed to the image of the Son (Rom 8:29).
- Awaiting the appearing of our Lord Jesus Christ, who will change our vile bodies to be conformed to His glorious body (Phil 3:20-21).
- Destined to see the Father's face (Rev 22:4) and dwell forever with Him in the New Jerusalem, in His Son, by His Spirit.

**This is the Gospel. This is the Person of Christ. This is the eternal purpose of the Father. This is who we are in Him.**

**All to the praise of the glory of His grace, wherein He has made us accepted in the Beloved** (Eph 1:6).

# **PART ONE — WHAT THIS MEANS FOR US INDIVIDUALLY**

## **The Foundational Reality**

Every one of us who is in Christ must first understand — not as abstract doctrine but as the settled truth of our being — that **we are not who we once were.**

The person who lived before conversion — the sinner, the rebel, the child of Adam, the one under condemnation — that person **was crucified with Christ** (Gal 2:20; Rom 6:6). The old man does not exist anymore in the Father's reckoning. The person who now bears your name is **a new creation** (2 Cor 5:17) — chosen in Christ before the foundation of the world (Eph 1:4), given to the Son by the Father (John 17:6, 24), redeemed by the Beloved's blood, indwelt by the Father's Spirit, and destined for glory.

**This is not aspiration. This is reality.**

The whole of your spiritual life — every discipline, every moment of faith, every trial, every joy, every act of obedience — is the daily working-out of what has already been decided about you in the Father's eternal purpose, accomplished by the Son's finished work, and applied by the Spirit's indwelling presence.

---

## **1. You Are Personally Loved by the Father**

Not as one among a nameless crowd. Not as a category or a project. **Personally, individually, by name.**

**The Father loves you with the same love He has for His Only-Begotten Son.**

### **John 17:23 — Geneva 1599:**

*"and hast loved them, as thou hast loved me."*


**Read that again slowly.** The Father loves you **as He loves the Son.** How is this possible? Because you are **in the Beloved** (Eph 1:6). The Father's love for His Son extends to all who are in Him.

This is not sentiment. It is settled fact. Before creation, the Father loved you in His Son. Nothing you have done can add to this love. Nothing you will do can diminish it. Nothing can separate you from it (Rom 8:38-39).

**Individually, this means:**

- **You do not need to earn the Father's favor.** You already have it in the Beloved.
- **You do not need to fear rejection.** You are accepted in the Beloved (Eph 1:6).
- **You do not need to prove your worth.** Your worth is settled by being in Him.
- **You do not need to compare yourself to other believers.** Each of us is loved with the whole love of the Father, in the Beloved.

**Live from this love, not for it.**

---

## **2. You Are Personally Indwelt by the Father Through the Son by the Spirit**

The Father — Eternal Spirit — has come to make His abode in you (John 14:23). The Son who bears the Father's Name lives in you (Gal 2:20; Col 1:27). The Spirit who was poured out through the glorified Son (Acts 2:33) seals you and dwells in you forever (Eph 1:13-14; 4:30; Rom 11:29).

**Your body — your mortal, weak, aging body — is the temple of the living God** (1 Cor 6:19; 2 Cor 6:16).

**Individually, this means:**

- **You are never alone.** The Father is with you, in you, closer than your own breath.
- **You are never without power.** The same Spirit that raised Jesus from the dead dwells in you (Rom 8:11).
- **You are never without wisdom.** The Spirit of truth guides you into all truth (John 16:13).
- **You are never without a Comforter.** The Paraclete is with you forever (John 14:16).
- **You are never without an Advocate.** The Son is at the Father's right hand, always living to make intercession for you (Heb 7:25; Rom 8:34).

The old covenant saints had the Spirit come upon them for tasks. **You have Him within you forever.** They looked forward; you look back to the finished work and forward to the coming glory — but with the indwelling reality now.

**Live in conscious awareness of His presence.** The Father is not distant. The Son is not absent. The Spirit is not merely a doctrine. **They dwell in you.**

---

## **3. You Are Personally Being Transformed**

The Father's purpose for you — **the specific reason He chose you** — is to be **conformed to the image of His Son** (Rom 8:29). Not merely saved from hell. Not merely forgiven. **Conformed to the image of the Beloved.**

Every circumstance of your life is being used by the Father toward this end. **Every one.** The good things equip you. The hard things refine you. The joyful things prepare you. The painful things deepen you. All things work together for good — the good defined as your conformity to Christ (Rom 8:28-29).

**Individually, this means:**

- **You are not what you will be.** You are a work in process. The Father is not finished with you.
- **Your failures do not define you.** They are moments in a longer arc that ends in glory.
- **Your growth is real, even when slow.** The Spirit's work is patient but certain.
- **Your circumstances are not accidents.** The Father is sovereignly weaving every thread.
- **Your suffering is not wasted.** It is producing an eternal weight of glory (2 Cor 4:17).

**Cooperate with the Father's transformative work.** Yield to the Spirit. Feed on the Word. Present your body daily as a living sacrifice. Reckon yourself dead to sin and alive to God. **The work is His; the yielding is yours.**

---

## **4. You Are Personally Called and Gifted for Purpose**

The Father did not save you merely to preserve you for heaven. He saved you to **serve Him now** in the specific place, time, and calling He has appointed for you.

### **Ephesians 2:10 — Geneva 1599:**

*"For we are his workmanship created in Christ Jesus unto good works, which God hath ordained, that we should walk in them."*


**You are His workmanship (ποίημα — His poem, His creation, His crafted work).** The Father has **prepared** (**προητοίμασεν** — pre-prepared) specific good works for you to walk in. Your life has a design, a purpose, a specific calling within the Father's larger plan.

**You are personally gifted** by the Spirit for the building up of the Body (1 Cor 12:7-11; Rom 12:6-8; 1 Pet 4:10-11). Whether teaching, serving, exhorting, giving, showing mercy, leading, or any other gift — the Father has equipped you specifically for your part.

**Individually, this means:**

- **Your life has meaning.** You are not a random arrangement of atoms; you are the Father's workmanship with prepared works to walk in.
- **You have a unique calling.** No one else can occupy your specific place in the Father's plan.
- **Your gifts are given for others.** They are not for your self-fulfillment but for the Body's edification.
- **Faithful stewardship matters.** You will give an account of what you have been entrusted with (2 Cor 5:10; 1 Cor 3:10-15).

**Discover your calling. Steward your gifts. Walk in the good works prepared for you.**

---

## **5. You Are Personally Destined for Glory**

Every believer, individually, is **destined for glory.**

### **Romans 8:30 — Geneva 1599:**

*"Moreover whom he predestinate *(predestinated)*, them also he called, and whom he called, them also he justified, and whom he justified, them he also glorified."*


**Five aorist verbs** — foreknew, predestined, called, justified, glorified. **Note: "glorified" is past tense.** In the Father's eternal purpose, your glorification is so certain it is already reckoned as accomplished.

**Individually, this means:**

- **Your future is settled.** The Father who began the good work in you will complete it (Phil 1:6).
- **Your body will be raised** and transformed to be conformed to the Son's glorious body (Phil 3:20-21).
- **You will see the Father's face** (Rev 22:4) — the face that no man has seen (John 1:18) — because in the risen Son you will behold Him.
- **You will reign** with Christ (2 Tim 2:12; Rev 22:5).
- **You will be like the Son** — for you will see Him as He is (1 John 3:2).

**Live with eternity in view.** This life is not the end of the story. The best is yet to come. The present sufferings are not worthy to be compared with the glory that shall be revealed (Rom 8:18).

---

## **6. You Are Personally Called to Suffer With Him — and Reign With Him**

We have already examined this fully under Romans 8:17 and the discussion of co-suffering. But it must be said personally: **you, in particular, are called to fill up your portion of the sufferings of Christ** (Col 1:24) — in whatever form the Father appoints for your life.

**Individually, this means:**

- **Do not be surprised by trials.** They are not exceptions to the Christian life; they are part of it.
- **Do not despair in suffering.** The Father is using every affliction to conform you to the Son.
- **Do not compare your sufferings with others'.** Each of us is called to a specific portion.
- **Rejoice, even in suffering.** You are being counted worthy to share in what the Beloved endured for you (Acts 5:41; 1 Pet 4:13).

**And know:** as you suffer with Him, you shall reign with Him (2 Tim 2:12). The path of the cross is the path to the crown.

---

## **7. You Are Personally Responsible for Faithful Perseverance**

Everything the Father has decreed for you, the Son has purchased for you, and the Spirit has begun in you — is **certain of completion.** You cannot lose what has been eternally given.

**But you are still called to walk faithfully.**

The indicative-imperative pattern applies to every day of your life: **because you are who you are in Christ, walk worthy** (Eph 4:1).

**Individually, this means:**

- **Keep the Word central.** Read it, meditate on it, memorize it, obey it.
- **Keep prayer central.** Communion with the Father through the Son by the Spirit.
- **Keep confession current.** Do not let unconfessed sin disrupt your fellowship.
- **Keep watch over your soul.** The adversary prowls; the flesh remains; the world presses.
- **Keep pressing forward.** Toward the mark, for the prize of the high calling of God in Christ Jesus (Phil 3:14).
- **Keep looking for His appearing.** The blessed hope (Titus 2:13).

**Perseverance is not earning salvation. It is walking faithfully in the salvation given.** The gift is free; the walk is required. Both are true, and both are held together by the Father's grace.
