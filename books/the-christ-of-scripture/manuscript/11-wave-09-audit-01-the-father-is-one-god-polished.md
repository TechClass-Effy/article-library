## **AUDIT OF FOUNDATION #1: The Father is the One God**

### Claim: There is one God, and that one God is the Father.

**Direct witnesses cited:**

- **Deut 6:4 LXX** — *"Κύριος ὁ Θεὸς ἡμῶν Κύριος εἷς ἐστιν"* — NETS: *"The Lord our God is one Lord."* ✓

- **Mark 12:29** — Jesus affirms the Shema *(Hebrew, "hear" — the name of Israel's one-God confession)* as the first commandment. ✓

- **John 17:3** — Jesus addresses the Father as **τὸν μόνον ἀληθινὸν Θεὸν** — Geneva: *"the only very God" (the only true God).* ✓

- **1 Cor 8:6** — Geneva: *"Yet unto us there is but one God, which is that Father… and one Lord Jesus Christ."* ✓

- **John 20:17** — Jesus calls the Father **Θεόν μου** — Geneva: *"to my God, and your God."* ✓

- **Eph 1:17** — Paul speaks of Geneva: *"the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, that Father of glory."* ✓

- **Isaiah 44-46** — repeated first-person declarations by the One God: NETS: *"besides me there is no god."* ✓

**Status: SOLIDLY ESTABLISHED.** Multiple direct, explicit witnesses, both OT and NT, both from God's own mouth and from Jesus' own lips. No inference required.
