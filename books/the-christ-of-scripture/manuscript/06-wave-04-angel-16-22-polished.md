## **A QUESTION ARISING — Who Is the Angel of the LORD?**

One of the most consequential threads in all of Scripture now opens — the appearances of **ὁ ἄγγελος Κυρίου** (*the Messenger of the LORD*). The texts must establish, by two-or-more witnesses, whether this Messenger is: (a) merely a created angel acting as God's spokesman, or (b) the One God Himself appearing, or (c) a distinct Person who speaks *as* God yet is *sent by* God — i.e., the pre-incarnate Son in one of His **ἔξοδοι** *(the goings-forth)* (Micah 5:2).

Per our maxims, we will not impose the answer; we will let Scripture deliver it.

---

## **THE FIRST APPEARANCE — Genesis 16: Hagar in the Wilderness**

> But the Lord God's angel found her by the spring of water in the wilderness, by the spring on the way to Sour *(Shur)*. And the angel of the Lord said to her, "Hagar, slave-girl of Sara *(Sarai)*, where are you coming from, and where are you going?" — **Genesis 16:7-8, LXX (NETS)**

> The angel of the Lord also said to her, "I will multitudinously *(greatly)* multiply your offspring, and it will not be counted for multitude." — **Genesis 16:10, LXX (NETS)**

### Observation of decisive importance

The Messenger says: **"I will multiply your seed."** This is a promise that **only God can make and fulfill** — and it is the **same promise** the LORD Himself makes directly to Abraham (Gen 13:16; 15:5; 22:17). The Messenger does not say *"The LORD will multiply"*; He says **"I will multiply."** He speaks in the first person *as God*.

> And Hagar called the name of the Lord who was speaking to her, "You-are-the-God-who-looksupon-me *(You are the God who sees me)*," because she said, "For truly I saw him face to face when he appeared to me." — **Genesis 16:13, LXX (NETS)**

### Observation of the utmost weight

Hagar identifies the Messenger as **Κύριος** — *the LORD* — and addresses Him as **Σὺ ὁ Θεός** — *"You are the God."* Furthermore, she marvels that she has **seen** Him and lived: **ἐνώπιον εἶδον ὀφθέντα μοι** — *"I have seen face-to-face the One who appeared to me."*

The narrator (Moses, by inspiration) does **not** correct her. Scripture *confirms* her identification: **"she called the name of the LORD who spoke to her: You are the God who sees me."**

So here is our first datum: **The Messenger speaks as God, is identified as God, and is seen — yet remains called "the Messenger of the LORD," not the LORD without qualification.**

## **THE SECOND APPEARANCE — Genesis 22: The Akedah**

> And the Lord's angel called him from the sky *(heaven)* and said to him, "Abraam *(Abraham)*, Abraam!" And he said, "Here I am." And he said, "Do not lay your hand on the youngster *(the boy)* nor do anything to him. For now I know that you do fear God, and for my sake you have not spared your beloved son." — **Genesis 22:11-12, LXX (NETS)**

### Observation

The Messenger says: **οὐκ ἐφείσω τοῦ υἱοῦ σου τοῦ ἀγαπητοῦ δι᾽ ἐμέ** — *"you did not spare your beloved son **for My sake**."*

But the entire test had been at the command of **ὁ Θεός** (v. 1): *"God tested Abraham."* So the One who is testing — God — is the same One **for whose sake** Abraham did not spare his son. The Messenger speaks as **the God** who commanded the test.

> And the Lord's angel called Abraam *(Abraham)* a second time from the sky *(heaven)*, saying, "By myself I have sworn, says the Lord: Inasmuch as you have carried out this matter and for my sake have not spared your beloved son, I will indeed bless you with blessings, and I will make your offspring as numerously numerous *(greatly multiplied)* as the stars of the sky and as the sand that is by the seashore, …" — **Genesis 22:15-18, LXX (NETS)**

### Observation of decisive weight

The Messenger says: **"Κατ᾽ ἐμαυτοῦ ὤμοσα, λέγει Κύριος"** — *"BY MYSELF I have sworn, says the LORD."*

This is identical to Isaiah 45:23 LXX: **κατ᾽ ἐμαυτοῦ ὀμνύω** — *"By Myself I swear,"* which we examined as the Father's exclusive self-oath. The Messenger speaks **as YHWH** swearing by Himself. He is not merely conveying a message — He is the One swearing, in the first person.

**Yet observe carefully** — the Messenger speaks **as YHWH swearing**, and yet the formula **λέγει Κύριος** *("says the LORD")* is used. This is the prophetic formula — the Messenger speaks for the LORD and as the LORD simultaneously. He is *of* the LORD and yet speaks *as* the LORD.

---

### The Apostolic confirmation — Hebrews 6:13-14

> For when God made the promise to Abraham, because he had no greater to swear by, he sware by himself, Saying, Surely I will abundantly bless thee and multiply thee marvelously. — **Hebrews 6:13-14, 1599 Geneva**

The author of Hebrews identifies the swearer as **ὁ Θεός** Himself. Therefore the **ἄγγελος Κυρίου** of Genesis 22 was speaking *as* God Himself swearing — yet without being called the LORD without qualification.
