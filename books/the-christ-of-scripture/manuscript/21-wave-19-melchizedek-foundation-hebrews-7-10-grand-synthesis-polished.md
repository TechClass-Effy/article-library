# **HEBREWS 7 — THE MELCHIZEDEK PRIESTHOOD**

## **Hebrews 7:1-3 — Geneva:**

### **1599 Geneva:**
*"For this Melchi-sedec *(Melchizedek)* was King of Salem, the Priest of the most high God, who met Abraham, as he returned from the slaughter of the Kings, and blessed him: To whom also Abraham gave the tithe of all things: who first is by interpretation King of righteousnes: after that, he is also King of Salem, that is, King of peace, Without father, without mother, without kinred, and hath neither beginning of his days, neither ende of life: but is likened unto the Son of God, and continueth a Priest for ever."*

---

### Observations of significant weight

**(1)** **βασιλεὺς δικαιοσύνης / βασιλεὺς εἰρήνης** — *"King of Righteousness / King of Peace."* These titles, drawn from the Hebrew etymology, foreshadow the dual prophecies of Christ as the Righteous One who brings peace.

**(2)** **ἀπάτωρ, ἀμήτωρ, ἀγενεαλόγητος** — *"without father, without mother, without genealogy."* The author of Hebrews uses Melchizedek's *narrative silence* about his lineage as a typological device. Melchizedek appears and disappears without genealogical context — making him a fitting *type* of an eternal Priest.

**(3)** **ἀφωμοιωμένος δὲ τῷ Υἱῷ τοῦ Θεοῦ** — *"having been made like unto the Son of God."* This is critical. **Melchizedek is made like the Son** — not the reverse. **The Son is the original; Melchizedek is the type.** This confirms the Son's pre-existence and priority — He is the reality of which Melchizedek is the foreshadow.

**(4)** **μένει ἱερεὺς εἰς τὸ διηνεκές** — *"He continues a Priest perpetually."* Of Melchizedek as narrative figure (and ultimately of the Son whom he prefigures).

---

## **Hebrews 7:11-17 — The Necessity of a New Priesthood**

### **1599 Geneva:**
*"If therefore perfection had been by the Priesthood of the Levites (for under it the Law was established to the people) what needed it furthermore, that another Priest should rise after the order of Melchi-sedec, and not to be called after the order of Aaron? For if the Priesthood be changed, then of necessitie must there be a change of the Law. For he of whom these things are spoken, perteineth unto another tribe, whereof no man serued at the altar. For it is euident, that our Lord sprung out of Iuda, concerning the which tribe Moses spake nothing, touching the Priesthood. And it is yet a more euident thing, because that after the similitude of Melchi-sedec, there is risen up another Priest, Which is not made Priest after the Law of the carnal commandment, but after the power of the endlesse life. For he testifieth thus, Thou art a Priest for ever, after the order of Melchi-sedec."*

---

### Observations

**(1)** **The Levitical priesthood could not bring perfection** — so a different priesthood was needed.

**(2)** **Jesus is from Judah, not Levi** — He cannot be a priest after the Aaronic order. He must be a priest of a different order.

**(3)** **κατὰ δύναμιν ζωῆς ἀκαταλύτου** — *"according to the power of an indestructible life."* The new Priest holds priesthood **by the power of a life that cannot be destroyed** — that is, **the resurrected life of the Son**. This is the key: His resurrection qualifies Him for this eternal priesthood.

**(4) Cross-witness on Jesus' tribe — Revelation 5:5:**

**1599 Geneva:** *"...the Lion which is of the tribe of Judah..."*

---

## **Hebrews 7:18-25 — Superior to the Old Priesthood**

### **1599 Geneva:**
*"For the commandment that went afore, is disannulled *(annulled)*, because of the weakness thereof, and unprofitableness. For the Law made nothing perfect, but the bringing in of a better hope made perfect, whereby we drawe neere unto God. And for as much as it is not without an othe (for these are made Priests without an othe: But this is made with an othe by him that said unto him, The Lord hath sworne, and will not repent, Thou art a Priest for ever, after the order of Melchi-sedec) By so much is Jesus made a surety of a better Testament. And among them many were made Priests, because they were not suffered to endure, by the reason of death. But this man, because he endureth ever, hath a Priesthood, which cannot pass from one to another. Wherefore, he is able also perfectly to save them that come unto God by him, seeing he ever liveth, to make intercession for them."*

---

### Observations of supreme importance

**(1)** **δι᾽ ἧς ἐγγίζομεν τῷ Θεῷ** — *"by which we draw near unto God."* The purpose of the new priesthood is **access to God [the Father].** The Son's priestly ministry is **for our access to the Father**.

**(2)** **The Father swore an oath** concerning Jesus' priesthood (citing Ps 110:4). The Father is the One swearing; the Son is the One sworn-to. Pattern preserved.

**(3)** **κρείττονος διαθήκης γέγονεν ἔγγυος Ἰησοῦς** — *"Jesus has become the guarantee of a better covenant."* He is the *guarantor* — He stands between the parties as security.

**(4)** **σῴζειν εἰς τὸ παντελὲς δύναται τοὺς προσερχομένους δι᾽ αὐτοῦ τῷ Θεῷ** — *"He is able to save completely those who come to God through Him."*

Observe the critical preposition: **δι᾽ αὐτοῦ τῷ Θεῷ** — *"through Him, to God [the Father]."* We come **to God** (the Father) **through Him** (the Son). The pattern of 1 Cor 8:6 is *exactly* preserved: the Father is the destination (**εἰς αὐτόν**); the Son is the way (**δι᾽ αὐτοῦ**).

**(5)** **πάντοτε ζῶν εἰς τὸ ἐντυγχάνειν ὑπὲρ αὐτῶν** — *"always living to make intercession for them."*

**This is the Son's present, ongoing ministry.** Right now, at this moment, the Son is **always living** at the Father's right hand, **always interceding** for those who come to the Father through Him. This is **what He is doing now.**

### **1599 Geneva:**

**Two witnesses (Heb 7:25 and Rom 8:34) establish the present intercessory ministry of the Son at the Father's right hand.**

Hebrews < 7 >"*

### Observations

**(1)** The Son is **holy, harmless, undefiled, separated from sinners** — fulfilling Isa 53:9 (*"He had done no violence, neither was any deceit in His mouth"*).

**(2)** **τοῦτο γὰρ ἐποίησεν ἐφάπαξ** — *"this He did once for all."* The Son's sacrifice of Himself is **once for all** — never to be repeated. This will become a key theme of chapter 10.

**(3)** **Υἱὸν εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα τετελειωμένον** — *"the Son, perfected forever."* The Son has been **perfected** (**τετελειωμένον** — completed, brought to His full appointed state) **forever.** This is His present condition.

# **HEBREWS 8 — THE BETTER COVENANT**

## **Hebrews 8:1-2 — Geneva:**

### **1599 Geneva:**
*"Now of the things which we have spoken, this is the sum, that we have such an high Priest, that sitteth at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in heavens, And is a minister of the Sanctuary, and of that true Tabernacle which the Lord pight, and not man."*

---

### Observations

**(1)** **ἐκάθισεν ἐν δεξιᾷ τοῦ θρόνου τῆς μεγαλωσύνης** — *"He sat down at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty"* — fulfilling Psalm 110:1.

Notice: the Son sat **at the right hand of the throne** — not *on* the throne of the Majesty. The Father's throne is central; the Son is at His right hand. This preserves the foundation.

**(2)** **τῶν ἁγίων λειτουργὸς καὶ τῆς σκηνῆς τῆς ἀληθινῆς** — *"a minister of the holy places and of the true Tabernacle."* The earthly tabernacle was a copy; the heavenly one is the **true** Tabernacle, pitched by **the Lord [the Father], not man.**

The author then cites **Jeremiah 31:31-34 LXX (Jeremiah 38:31-34 LXX)** in full — one of the longest OT citations in the NT.

*"Behold, days are coming, quoth *(says)* the Lord, and I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel and the house of Iouda *(Judah)*. It will not be like the covenant that I made with their fathers in the day when I took them by their hand to bring them out of the land of Egypt, because they did not abide in my covenant, and I was unconcerned for them, quoth the Lord, because this is the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel after those days, quoth the Lord. **The Son's high-priestly ministry is the ministry of this New Covenant.** He is its **guarantor** (Heb 7:22) and its **mediator** (Heb 8:6, 9:15, 12:24).

# **HEBREWS 9 — THE ONCE-FOR-ALL SACRIFICE**

## **Hebrews 9:11-14 — Geneva:**

### **1599 Geneva:**
*"But Christ being come an high Priest of good things to come, by a greater and a more perfect Tabernacle, not made with hands, that is, not of this building, Neither by the blood of goates and calues: but by his own blood entered he in once unto the holy place, and obtained eternal redemption for us. For if the blood of bulles and of goates, and the ashes of an heifer, sprinkling them that are vncleane, sanctifieth as touching the purifying of the flesh, How much more shall the blood of Christ which through the eternal Spirit offered himself without fault to God, purge your conscience from dead workes, to serue the living God?"*

---

### Observations of supreme weight

**(1)** **εἰσῆλθεν ἐφάπαξ εἰς τὰ ἅγια** — *"He entered once for all into the holy places."* The Son **entered** the heavenly Tabernacle **once for all** — a completed action, not repeated.

**(2)** **διὰ δὲ τοῦ ἰδίου αἵματος** — *"by His own blood."* Not the blood of animals; **His own blood**.

**(3)** **αἰωνίαν λύτρωσιν εὑράμενος** — *"having found / obtained eternal redemption."* Already obtained — completed.

**(4)** **ἑαυτὸν προσήνεγκεν ἄμωμον τῷ Θεῷ** — *"He offered Himself without blemish to God [the Father]."* The Son is both the **Offerer** and the **Offering** — *and* He offers Himself **to the Father.** The Father is the Recipient of the offering. Pattern preserved.

**(5)** **εἰς τὸ λατρεύειν Θεῷ ζῶντι** — *"to serve the living God."* The purpose: that we may serve **God** (the Father).

---

## **Hebrews 9:15 — Mediator of the New Covenant**

### **1599 Geneva:**
*"And for this cause is he the Mediator of the new Testament, that through death which was for the redemption of the transgressions that were in the former Testament, they which were called, might receive the promise of eternal inheritance."*

---

### Cross-witness — 1 Timothy 2:5:

### **1599 Geneva:**
*"For there is one God, and one Mediator between God and man, which is the man Christ Jesus,"*

---

### Observation of foundational weight

**1 Timothy 2:5 is decisive for our entire study.** Paul declares:
- **εἷς Θεός** — *one God* (the Father, per our foundation).
- **εἷς μεσίτης** — *one Mediator* — between God and men.
- **ἄνθρωπος Χριστὸς Ἰησοῦς** — *the Man Christ Jesus*.

**The Mediator is the Man Christ Jesus.** Paul, even after His exaltation, calls Him **ἄνθρωπος** — *Man*. The mediation is **between God [the Father] and men** — and the Mediator stands between as **a Man**.

This is a critical text in our study because it preserves both:
- **The Father as the One God** (per the Shema and Isa 44-46).
- **The Son as the exalted Man** who mediates between God and men.

The Son's **mediation** at the Father's right hand is **as Man** — bearing the humanity He assumed in incarnation forever, now glorified.

## **Hebrews 9:24-28 — Christ Appeared Before the Father**

### **1599 Geneva:**
*"For Christ is not entered into the holy places that are made with hands, which are similitudes *(copies)* of the true Sanctuary: but is entered into very heaven, to appeare now in ye sight of God for us, Not that he should offer himself often, as the high Priest entered into the Holy place every year with other blood, (For then must he have often suffred since the foundation of the world) but now in the end of the world hath he been made manifest, once to put away sin by the sacrifice of him self. And as it is appointed unto men that they shall once die, and after that cometh the judgment: So Christ was once offered to take away the sins of many, and unto them that looke for him, shall he appeare the second time without sin unto saluation. Hebrews < 9 >"*

---

### Observations of decisive importance for the Son's present ministry

**(1)** **εἰς αὐτὸν τὸν οὐρανόν** — *"into heaven itself."* The Son entered the **real heaven** — not a copy. His ministry is in heaven now.

**(2)** **νῦν ἐμφανισθῆναι τῷ προσώπῳ τοῦ Θεοῦ ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν** — *"NOW to appear before the face of God for us."*

This is the **single most concise statement of the Son's present ministry:**
- **νῦν** — *now* — at this very moment.
- **ἐμφανισθῆναι** — *to appear, to be manifest.*
- **τῷ προσώπῳ τοῦ Θεοῦ** — *before the face of God [the Father].*
- **ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν** — *on our behalf.*

**The Son is, right now, appearing before the Father's face on our behalf.** This is the present, ongoing reality of His high-priestly ministry. He stands as the Man Christ Jesus before the Father, presenting His finished sacrifice and interceding for those who come to the Father through Him.

**(3)** **ἅπαξ προσενεχθεὶς εἰς τὸ πολλῶν ἀνενεγκεῖν ἁμαρτίας** — *"having been offered once for all to bear the sins of many."* Direct echo of Isaiah 53:12. Once-for-all.

**(4)** **ἐκ δευτέρου χωρὶς ἁμαρτίας ὀφθήσεται** — *"He shall appear a second time, without sin."* His **second appearing** is foretold here — **without sin** (because sin was dealt with at the first appearing) — **εἰς σωτηρίαν** — *unto salvation* — for those who eagerly await Him.

This is the framework for our future treatment of His return.

## **Hebrews 10:5-10 — The Son's Body Prepared**

### **1599 Geneva:**
*"Wherefore when he cometh into the world, he saith, Sacrifice and offering thou wouldest not: but a body hast thou ordeined me. In burnt offerings, and sin offerings thou hast had no pleasure. Then I said, Lo, I come (In the beginning of the booke it is written of me) that I should do thy will, O God."*

---

### Critical text — Psalm 40:6-8 LXX (Ps 39:7-9 LXX) cited

This is a direct citation of **Psalm 39:7-9 LXX**:

### **Psalm 39:7-9 (LXX = MT 40:6-8) — NETS:**
*"Sacrifice and offering you did not want, but ears you fashioned for me. Whole burnt offering and one for sin you did not request. Then I said, “Look, I have come; in a scroll of a book it is written of me. To do your will, O my God, I desired—and your law, within my belly.”"*

### **1599 Geneva (Psalm 40:6-8, MT) — a second recourse:**
*"Sacrifice and offering thou didest not desire: (for mine eares hast thou prepared) burnt offering and sin offering hast thou not required. Then said I, Lo, I come: for in the rolle of the booke it is written of me, I desired to do thy good will, O my God: yea, thy Law is within mine heart."*

---

### Observation of considerable importance

**Note the textual difference:**
- **LXX:** **σῶμα δὲ κατηρτίσω μοι** — *"a body You prepared for Me."*
- **Hebrew (followed by the 1599 Geneva):** *"mine ears hast thou prepared"* (or "digged/opened").

**The author of Hebrews uses the LXX reading** — **σῶμα δὲ κατηρτίσω μοι** — *"a body You prepared for Me."* Per our maxim #1 and our commitment to the LXX, this is the God-breathed reading the Apostle uses.

This text is striking: it is presented as **the Son speaking** as He **enters the world** (**εἰσερχόμενος εἰς τὸν κόσμον**). The Son, on the eve of His incarnation, says to the Father: *"You did not want sacrifices and offerings — but a body You prepared for Me. Behold, I come to do Your will, O God."*

**The Son speaks of His own coming into the world as His act of obedience to the Father's will.** The Father prepared the body; the Son took it; the Son's purpose was to do the Father's will. This is the highest expression of the Father-Source / Son-Sent pattern, applied to the Incarnation itself.

## **Hebrews 10:11-14 — Seated Forever**

### **1599 Geneva:**
*"And every Priest standeth daily ministering, and oft times offereth one manner of offering, which can never take away sins: But this man after he had offered one sacrifice for sins, sitteth for ever at the right hand of God, And from hencefoorth tarieth, till his enemies be made his footestoole. For with one offering hath he consecrated *(brought to full completion)* for ever them that are sanctified."*

---

### Observations of foundational weight

**(1)** **ἕστηκεν** — *"stands"* — the Levitical priests **stand** daily. **ἐκάθισεν** — *"sat down"* — the Son **sat down** after His one offering. The contrast is intentional: the seated Priest has finished His work; the standing priests have not.

**(2)** **εἰς τὸ διηνεκὲς ἐκάθισεν ἐν δεξιᾷ τοῦ Θεοῦ** — *"forever sat down at the right hand of God."* The Son is enthroned forever — **at the right hand of God [the Father]**. Psalm 110:1 is again confirmed.

**(3)** **τὸ λοιπὸν ἐκδεχόμενος ἕως τεθῶσιν οἱ ἐχθροὶ αὐτοῦ ὑποπόδιον τῶν ποδῶν αὐτοῦ** — *"henceforth waiting until His enemies be made His footstool."* Direct citation of Psalm 110:1.

The Son is **waiting** at the Father's right hand. This is His **present posture** — seated, interceding, waiting — **until the Father makes His enemies His footstool.** The Father is the One who acts to subdue the enemies; the Son waits.

This is a crucial detail for our study of His return: **the Father determines the timing**; the Son waits in expectation. This is consistent with Acts 1:7 (*"It is not for you to know the times or the seasons, which the Father hath put in his own power"*) and Mark 13:32 (*"neither knoweth the Son, but the Father"*).

**(4)** **μιᾷ γὰρ προσφορᾷ τετελείωκεν εἰς τὸ διηνεκὲς τοὺς ἁγιαζομένους** — *"by one offering He has perfected forever those who are being sanctified."* **One offering. Forever perfected.** The work is finished.

## **What This Establishes About His Person — Carefully**

**(1)** **The Son is, *now*, a living Man** — exalted, glorified, but still **the Man Christ Jesus** (1 Tim 2:5). He did not shed His humanity at the resurrection; He took it with Him into heaven.

**(2)** **He bears the marks of His suffering even now.** The risen Christ appeared with pierced hands and feet and side (John 20:25-27); the Lamb in the Apocalypse appears **as having been slain** (Rev 5:6). The crucified Man is the enthroned Priest.

**(3)** **He acts always in dependence on and submission to the Father.** Even at the Father's right hand, He waits *for the Father to subdue His enemies*. His enthronement does not erase the order: the Father is the One supreme God; the Son rules **at His right hand** by His appointment.

**(4)** **He is the only Mediator** between the Father and humanity. No priest stands beside Him. No Mary, no saint, no apostle, no minister mediates between God and men — **only the Man Christ Jesus.** This is critical doctrine, and it is firmly established.

**(5)** **His ministry is *for us*.** Every act of His present session — His appearing, His interceding, His waiting, His opening the way — is **ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν** (*on our behalf*). The Son's enthronement is not a private glory; it is an active ministry for those who come to the Father through Him.

## **A Critical Observation Preserving Our Foundation**

Notice that throughout Hebrews 7-10, the Father remains **ὁ Θεός** — *the God* — to whom:
- The Son offered Himself (9:14: **τῷ Θεῷ**).
- The Son sat down at His right hand (10:12: **ἐν δεξιᾷ τοῦ Θεοῦ**).
- We come through the Son (7:25: **τῷ Θεῷ**).
- The Father's house is overseen by the Son (10:21: **ἐπὶ τὸν οἶκον τοῦ Θεοῦ**).
- The Father swore the oath establishing the Son's priesthood (7:21).
- The Father prepared the body for the Son's incarnation (10:5).
- The Father's will is what the Son came to do (10:7-9).
- The Father is the One who will make the Son's enemies His footstool (10:13).
