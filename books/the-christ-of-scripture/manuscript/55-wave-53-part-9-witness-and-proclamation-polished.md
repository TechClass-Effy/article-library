# **PART 9 — WITNESS AND PROCLAMATION**

The believer is called to bear witness to the Son before the world.

## **The Mandate**

### **Matthew 28:19-20 — Geneva 1599:**

*"Go therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the Name of the Father, and the Son, and the holy Ghost, Teaching them to observe all things, whatsoever I have commanded you: and lo, I am with you alway, until the end of the world, Amen."*

The Great Commission belongs to every believer, in its various spheres of application.

---

## **The Posture of the Witness**

### **1 Peter 3:15 — Geneva 1599:**

*"But sanctify the Lord God in your hearts: and be ready always to give an answer to every man that asketh you a reason of the hope that is in you, with meekness and reverence,"*

---

### Observations

**(1) Sanctify the Lord in your hearts** — give Him the place of supremacy first internally. This is the foundation of all witness.

**(2) Be ready** — be prepared to give an answer. The believer should know the Gospel well enough to articulate it.

**(3) For the hope that is in you** — the witness centers on **the hope** — the certain expectation of glory through Christ.

**(4) With meekness and reverence** — not arrogantly, not combatively. The witness is winsome, humble, reverent.

---

## **The Believer's Witness**

The believer witnesses in multiple ways:

### **By Life**

### **Matthew 5:16 — Geneva 1599:**

*"Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven."*

The believer's life — his integrity, his love, his joy in trial, his honesty, his faithfulness — is itself a witness to the world.

### **By Word**

### **Romans 10:14-15 — Geneva 1599:**

*"But how shall they call on him, in whom they have not believed? and how shall they believe in him, of whom they have not heard? and how shall they hear without a preacher? And how shall they preach, except they be sent? as it is written, How beautiful are the feet of them which bring glad tidings of peace, and bring glad tidings of good things!"*

Faith comes by hearing (Rom 10:17). The Gospel must be proclaimed verbally. **Lives alone are insufficient** — the explicit Gospel must be spoken.

### **By Service**

Acts of mercy, compassion, and service open doors for the Gospel and embody it visibly.

---

## **The Content of the Witness**

The Gospel itself — the good news that:
- The Father loved the world and sent His Beloved Son.
- The Son died for our sins and rose again the third day.
- He is now at the Father's right hand, the appointed Lord and Christ.
- Whoever believes in Him receives forgiveness and eternal life.
- He will return to judge the living and the dead.
- All who are in Him are children of God, indwelt by the Spirit, destined for glory.

This is the core. The believer should know how to articulate it briefly and clearly.

---

## **Practical Implications**

**1. Live a life consistent with your message.** Hypocrisy nullifies witness.

**2. Be ready to speak when opportunities arise.** Pray for opportunities; recognize them; take them.

**3. Be honest about your own journey.** Personal testimony is powerful — *"I was blind, now I see"* (John 9:25).

**4. Be patient with the lost.** Conversion is the Father's work; you are a faithful witness.

**5. Pray for the lost.** Especially those near to you — family, neighbors, coworkers.

**6. Support the proclamation of the Gospel** — pray for and give to those engaged in missions and evangelism beyond your immediate reach.

**7. Do not be ashamed of the Gospel** (Rom 1:16). It is the power of God unto salvation.
