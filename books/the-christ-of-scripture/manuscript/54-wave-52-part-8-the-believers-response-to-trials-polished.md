# **PART 8 — THE BELIEVER'S RESPONSE TO TRIALS**

The believer encounters varied trials. The Christian's response is distinct from the world's.

## **The General Pattern: Joy in Trial**

### **James 1:2-4 — Geneva 1599:**

*"My brethren, count it exceeding joy, when ye fall into divers temptations, Knowing that the trying of your faith bringeth forth patience, And let patience have her perfect work, that ye may be perfect and entire, lacking nothing."*

---

### Observations

**(1) Count it all joy** — not because trials are pleasant, but because of what they accomplish.

**(2) Trials produce ὑπομονή** — *patience, endurance, steadfast perseverance.* The believer's character is forged through trial.

**(3) Patience produces maturity** — *"perfect and entire, lacking nothing."*

The believer's response: **joy**, because the Father is using the trial for His perfecting work.

---

## **The Different Kinds of Trials**

### **Persecution for Righteousness**

We examined this fully in our earlier discussion of suffering with Christ. The response: **rejoice** (Matt 5:11-12), **bless** (Rom 12:14), **count it joy** to be counted worthy to suffer for the Name (Acts 5:41).

### **Affliction in the Body**

Illness, weakness, pain, the limitations of mortal life. The response: **trust the Father's purpose**, **seek prayer** (James 5:14-16), **submit to the Father's wisdom**, **endure with hope** of the resurrection (Rom 8:23).

### **Loss and Bereavement**

The death of loved ones, the loss of relationships, the breaking of dear things. The response: **grieve, but not as those without hope** (1 Thess 4:13). Christian grief is real; it is also held within the certainty of resurrection.

### **Economic Hardship**

Job loss, financial pressure, poverty. The response: **trust the Father's provision** (Matt 6:25-34), **work diligently** (2 Thess 3:10-12), **be content** (Phil 4:11-13), **seek the kingdom first** (Matt 6:33).

### **Relational Trials**

Conflict, betrayal, loneliness, marital difficulty. The response: **pray for those who hurt you** (Matt 5:44), **forgive as Christ forgave** (Col 3:13), **seek peace as far as it depends on you** (Rom 12:18), **trust the Lord for outcomes you cannot control.**

### **Inner Trials**

Depression, anxiety, doubt, dryness in prayer. The response: **bring it honestly before the Lord** (the Psalms model this), **rest in His unchanging promises** even when feelings vary, **seek the help of trusted brethren**, **be patient with the slow work of the Spirit in dark seasons.**

### **Temptation**

Already discussed. The response: flee or resist as appropriate; look for the way of escape.

---

## **The Sustaining Truths**

In every trial, the believer holds fast to certain truths:

1. **The Father is sovereign** — nothing reaches you outside His permission.
2. **The Father is loving** — He does not afflict from caprice or cruelty.
3. **All things work for good** (Rom 8:28) — even this.
4. **Christ has been there** — He sympathizes with our weaknesses (Heb 4:15).
5. **The Spirit indwells** — strengthening, comforting, interceding.
6. **The trial is temporary** — *"light affliction which is but for a moment"* (2 Cor 4:17).
7. **The glory is coming** — incomparably greater than the present sufferings (Rom 8:18).
8. **Nothing separates us from His love** (Rom 8:38-39).

---

## **Practical Implications**

**1. Do not be surprised by trials** (1 Pet 4:12). They are normal in the Christian life.

**2. Bring trials to the Father in prayer.** Do not bear them alone.

**3. Look for the lessons the Father is teaching.** What patience is He growing? What dependence? What conformity to the Son?

**4. Seek the help of the Body.** Bear one another's burdens.

**5. Use the trial as an opportunity to witness.** Many have come to faith through observing Christians under trial.

**6. Keep eternal perspective.** This life is not the end of the story.

**7. Be honest with God in your grief.** The Psalms model this. You do not have to pretend.
